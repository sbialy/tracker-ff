# Changelog

All notable changes to the **Polyend Tracker Community Edition (CE)** firmware across **Original Tracker** (`MK66`), **Tracker+** and **Tracker Mini** (`IMX`) are documented in this file.

---

## [2.0.0] - 2026-11-26 - tracker-ff 2.0.0 (original Tracker, Tracker+ and Tracker Mini)

Marks: [+] new, [~] changed, [*] fixed, [!] known issue.

Updating from 1.4.0: see [`UPGRADING.md`](UPGRADING.md).

Version 2.0.0 is the first tracker-ff release for all three trackers. The original Tracker (MK66), the Tracker+ and the Tracker Mini (IMX) are built from the same source and share the project features below.

### Tracker

1. [+] **Tracker+ and Tracker Mini.** Both IMX trackers support everything of tracker-ff 1.4.0 and the new features of this release while the original Tracker gets the stereo track pairs, the USB audio 8+3 mode and a USB drive mode of its own (Details, Tracker 1).
2. [+] **Capture.** Every note you play is kept and button 3 of the pattern editor writes what you played into the track you are on with one Undo, and a take longer than the pattern goes on into new patterns of the song, on by default on all three trackers (Details, Tracker 2).
3. [+] **Tuning.** The Project Settings page gets a Tuning group with notes per period, period size, base key and frequency and Scala files (`.scl`) from `/Tunings` (Details, Tracker 3).
4. [+] **Tables.** Every sample instrument and synth part gets a Table page of 16 rows with a transpose, a volume and one FX, started by a note or by the new `TBL` step FX and set with an FX picker (Details, Tracker 4 and 5).
5. [+] **Stereo track pairs and stereo recording (new on the original Tracker).** Linked track pairs play stereo samples as one voice and Line in, Radio and USB audio record stereo takes (Details, Tracker 6 and 7).
6. [+] **Reverse, Oscillator and Repitch for samples.** Three new play modes and a `Repitch` row that fits the sample to a set number of steps (Details, Tracker 8).
7. [+] **MPE out.** One MPE lower zone for the MIDI instruments, with every note on its own member channel (Details, Tracker 9).
8. [+] **MIDI Program Change in.** A Program Change over USB or the MIDI In jack picks the bank slot of a synth part (Details, Tracker 10).
9. [+] **Nine more chords for the MIDI Chord FX.** The table grows from 68 to 77 chords (Details, Tracker 11).
10. [+] **Shift + FX1 and Shift + FX2 select FX3 and FX4.** With 4 FX Slots on, holding Shift and pressing FX1 or FX2 in the pattern editor goes straight to FX3 or FX4 without the tap flip (Details, Tracker 12).
11. [~] **`Plugins/Ext/extras.plg` replaces `modit.plg`.** Copy the file from the zip onto the card of the original Tracker for MOD and IT import and export, custom themes and the factory self test (Details, Tracker 13).
12. [*] **Line in crackle at a slow tempo (original Tracker).** An external synth on the Line in no longer crackles or breaks up at a slow tempo, mostly after Stop then Play, and its sound arrives 3 ms later (Details, Tracker 14).
13. [*] **Dense MIDI input no longer stalls the audio.** Dense MIDI notes or a very fast external clock over USB or DIN no longer make the sound drop out or reset the unit (Details, Tracker 15).
14. [*] **More upstream fixes from tracker-ce.** Song and pattern export no longer rewrites its file on every flush, the project sample export no longer damages the files it writes, corrupt project, pattern and instrument files no longer crash or overrun, radio frequency steps and tuning errors are handled better, the Sample Editor and the Delay page get a few small fixes and a Performance mode Sample start or Sample end that ends while a Position FX sounds keeps the end point of the instrument (Details, Tracker 16).
15. [*] **USB audio from the computer no longer breaks up (original Tracker).** The stream from the computer is asynchronous with a feedback endpoint now, a bus reset sets the audio settings back and 8+3 mono leaves the input out (Details, Tracker 17).
16. [*] **The USB drive shows as Polyend Tracker SD card (original Tracker).** The vendor is Polyend now (Details, Tracker 18).
17. [*] **Project Settings value column (all three trackers).** With a Scala file or a tuning picked, the Stereo pairs row (original Tracker) or the Scale snap row showed the tuning text instead of its own (Details, Tracker 19).
18. [!] **A held note does not follow a tuning change.** A held note keeps its pitch and the next note takes the new tuning (Details, Tracker 20).
19. [!] **Tuning on firmware 1.4.0.** A project saved with a tuning plays in 12 notes on 1.4.0 and keeps its tuning lines (Details, Tracker 21).
20. [~] **One Project Settings page.** The nine project rows of Config > Project Settings (Pattern divider, Anti-aliasing, Limiter mode, Sends mode, Extra headroom, Snap to zero, Notes scale, Root note, Notes layout) moved to the Project Settings page of the File screen (F7) and Performance presets moved to Config > General (Details, Tracker 22).

### Synth

1. [+] **New note action (original Tracker, Tracker+ and Mini for plugin engines).** A synth part gets a `New note` setting for what happens to the note a track still holds when the next one starts (Details, Synth 1).
2. [+] **MPE in on the Synth play page.** A ROLI Seaboard RISE 2 or any MPE controller plays with a pitch bend and a pressure for each note and the slide on the Mod wheel macro and on the Tracker+ and the Mini a part that plays a plugin engine takes the bend and the pressure of each note too (Details, Synth 2).
3. [+] **Part destinations for Synth Automation.** A slot can drive the pan, gain, reverb send or delay send of its synth part (Details, Synth 3).
4. [+] **NNA step FX (original Tracker, Tracker+ and Mini for plugin engines).** A step FX that sets the New note action for the note on its step only (Details, Synth 4).

### Details

#### Tracker

1. [+] **Tracker+ and Tracker Mini.** The two IMX trackers are built from the same source as the original Tracker and get these project features of the original Tracker, the ones of 1.2.0 to 1.4.0 and the new ones of this release: the Project Settings page and `project.mtff`, 4 FX Slots, Scale snap and Snap FX, Follow chord, Random seed, groove, Parts, the Features page, send ducking, master inserts, Soft Knee, Patch FX for the synth parts, the Synth Automation page with the part destinations, Tables, the Table FX picker, Tuning with Scala files for sample tracks, MPE out, the Synth play page with A/B and REC, MPE in on the Synth play page, the nine new chords and MIDI Program Change in. The Synth play page is the one of 1.4.0 on all three trackers, with REC recording a mono take of the master output into the free sample memory as on the original Tracker. On Tracker+ and Mini the pair of Synth A and Synth B plays 4+4 voices because the stock voice pool is 8 for all parts (the original Tracker plays 8+8, PLK 4+4), Synth B starts Off and the Mini opens the page from box 12 of the Menu popup. The synth parts there keep the stock engines. The image also reads the plugin synth engines (`.plg` files in `/Plugins/Synth` on the card): they are listed after the stock engines in the Model list and an engine is loaded into memory when a synth part picks it (the `chip.plg` file is not used there, the stock CHIP engine plays). A plugin engine there follows the pitch bend (the bend wheel of the Synth play page, the Table transpose and a pitch bend from MIDI in, 2 semitones as the stock engines) and the project tuning, as on the original Tracker. A `0 - MIDI Chord` step on a part that plays JU6 or ALCO at Para plays the chord as one paraphonic note, with the `u - Strum` FX next to it, as on the original Tracker. The New note action of a part that plays a plugin engine works there too, set on the `New note` block of the Synth page (the column right of Delay send, only for a part that plays a plugin engine, the stock engines keep Release and the block is hidden for them), for one note with the `Q - NNA` step FX, or kept in a project from the original Tracker, and an Off of one track ends only that track's note when two tracks play the same pitch on the part. The stock engines there keep Release. On the Synth play page such a part takes the pitch bend and the pressure of each note of an MPE controller. Stereo recording and editing, USB audio with more output channels than the 8+3 mode and the USB drive are part of Polyend's firmware on the Tracker+ and the Mini, so this release adds on the original Tracker only the stereo track pairs, USB audio 8+3 and its own USB drive mode. The contents of the card module `extras.plg` (MOD and IT import and export, custom themes, the factory self test and the take logic of Capture) are linked into the Tracker+ and Mini images, so those two need no card file for them. The Config > Firmware page of all three reads 2.0.0.
2. [+] **Capture.** Every note you play on the pads and over MIDI is kept, with nothing to switch on. A note that starts 30 seconds after the last held note ended starts a new take. Button 3 under the screen in the green mode of the pattern editor writes the take (the notes played since the last gap) straight into the track you are on, with the quantise, micro timing and velocity options of the live record. The track is the armed one when exactly one is armed, else the track the first note came in on over a track channel, else the cursor track. The first note lands on step 1 and the others by their timing. A press works with the sequencer running or stopped, with the sequencer stopped the first note you played is step 1 and the others follow by their timing at the project tempo. Only Rec refuses it (the popup says to stop recording first). One Undo takes a press back. A press does not use the take up: pressing again in the same pattern writes the same notes on the same steps and pressing in another pattern writes the same take there. A take longer than the pattern fills it from step 1 and the rest goes into new empty patterns (the first pattern numbers nobody uses) that are put into the song right after the slot of the pattern, 16 patterns at most, and one Undo takes the press and the new patterns back (Redo stays empty). Notes beyond the 16 patterns are left out and the popup counts them. A second press on the same pattern writes the same notes on the same steps again and a longer take grows the block it made. There is no spill while a Part plays, while Performance mixes tracks or when the pattern has no slot in the song, so a note past the end of the pattern is left out there. The write is paced so the sample streams keep their reads, and a long spill (a take of 16 patterns) holds the screen and the buttons for about 3.5 to 3.8 seconds on a project with eight streamed stereo samples while the sound keeps playing, about half a second on a project without streams. The Copy buffer is not touched. Holding button 3 forgets the take. A small square in the title bar shows the state of the take: a hollow dim outline when there is no take, filled green when a press writes the take, a dim filled square when a press would not write it now (while Rec is on) and a hollow green outline when the take is older than the 30 second gap (a press still writes it). The take is also kept in the file `Capture/take.cap` on the card. The Capture row on the Config > Features page switches it off. It works on all three trackers. On the original Tracker the take logic is part of `extras.plg` (copy it from the zip into `Plugins/Ext`), without the file button 3 names `extras.plg` and nothing is captured.
3. [+] **Tuning.** The Project Settings page (File screen, F7) has a Tuning group with a few rows, a set of fine rows, a `Scale file` row and a `Note names` row. `Notes/period` sets how many notes make one period (1 to 128, default 12). `Period` sets the size of the period (2/1 is 1200 cents, with presets and a `Custom` value that the fine rows set to 0.001 cent). `1/1 key` is the key that sounds the base pitch and `1/1 Hz` its frequency (default 261.6256, with presets and fine rows). `Scale file` lists the Scala files (`.scl`) in the `/Tunings` folder of the card. Pick one and its note count and period replace the first two rows, so a just intonation or Bohlen-Pierce scale of up to 128 notes plays on every key. A file that is not a valid Scala scale is refused with the reason and the tuning stays as it was. The project keeps a copy of the scale, so it opens on a card without the file. The tuning changes the pitch of sample tracks on all three trackers, of synth parts on the original Tracker and of synth parts that play a plugin engine on the Tracker+ and the Mini (the PERC and T-Type kits stay untuned). The stock engines of the Tracker+ and the Mini stay in 12 notes. A note that is already playing keeps its pitch and the next note takes the new tuning. While the tuning is not 12 notes per period the note names show as degrees, the pad board reaches every key up to 127 and the octave shift moves by one period. `Note names` can switch an equal division of the octave that has a usable fifth (17, 19, 22, 24 and 31 notes among them) to ups and downs names such as ^C4 or vD2. Scale snap works on the degrees of the tuning, with the project scale spelled on the nominals of the division (a Scala file keeps Snap off). Finetune counts cents in a tuning and Tune keeps counting keys. MIDI Chord and Arp intervals keep their shape: a major third plays the key at the same fraction of the period. A key that a chord or the Arp would play twice plays once when the tuning has fewer than 12 notes (Follow chord, chord capture and the paraphonic synth engines stay in 12 notes). A change is refused while the sequencer plays. A project with the default tuning has no Tuning line in `project.mtff` and plays as before. Five example files are in `Tunings/` of the zip.
4. [+] **Tables (Table lite).** The instrument editor gets a Table page, the last page of a sample instrument and of a synth part. A table has 16 rows and each row holds N (a transpose), V (a volume in percent) and one FX with its value. Tic sets the length of a row, Len how many rows run and Mode Loop or Once. A table starts with a note of the instrument (from a step, a pad or MIDI) or with the new `TBL` step FX on the track. Releasing a pad or a MIDI note ends a table that loops. F5 copies, F6 pastes and F7 clears the selected rows or the whole table. The tables are stored in the project (`project.mtff`). On the Tracker+ and the Mini a synth part takes the transpose of a row as a pitch bend of up to 2 semitones, with a stock engine and with a plugin engine, and does not take its volume.
5. [+] **FX picker on the Table page.** Holding Fx, Vol or F8 on the grid opens a panel with every FX. Pick one with the encoder, the arrows or a pad and release the key to set it. The FX cell shows the letter and the full name of the FX.
6. [+] **Stereo track pairs (new on the original Tracker).** The Project Settings page has a Stereo pairs group with one row per pair (tracks 1+2, 3+4, 5+6 and 7+8). A linked pair plays a stereo sample as one stereo voice. The odd track plays it and the even track stays silent and keeps its pattern data. The Mixer draws a pair as two strips under one label (the left meter in the first slot and the right meter in the second) with one volume and one mute for the pair, and every track keeps its own slot. Mute and Performance act on the pair as one and the song stems of a pair are a real stereo file. Links change only while the sequencer is stopped. An unlinked project plays and saves as before.
7. [+] **Stereo recording, editing and sends (new on the original Tracker).** With Config > Line in channel set to Stereo and a project that has linked pairs, a Line in or Radio recording is a stereo take (the radio decodes FM stereo for it) and the Sample Editor edits both channels of a stereo sample. A linked pair sends its stereo image to the delay and the reverb, not only its mid. The Sample Recorder has a fifth source, USB: the computer plays stereo 16 bit 44.1 kHz audio into the unit over the USB audio device and the recorder takes it with gain and a level meter. A project without a linked pair records, edits and sends as before and the mics stay mono.
8. [+] **Reverse, Oscillator and Repitch for samples.** The play mode of a sample instrument gets `1-Shot Rev`, `Pingpong Rev` and `Oscillator`. The Sends page of the instrument Params gets a `Repitch` row that makes the played part of the sample last a set number of steps at any tempo. `1-Shot Rev` and `Pingpong Rev` play the sample backwards from its End point and the step FX Reverse on such an instrument plays it forward. `Oscillator` loops one cycle between the loop points at the pitch of the note (C-5 sounds 523.25 Hz whatever the length of the cycle) and ignores the Start point and Repitch. `Repitch` is Off or 1 to 255 steps. It follows a tempo change while the note sounds and it does not apply to Wavetable, Slice, Beat Slice and Granular (the row shows `-`). The three modes and Repitch play from RAM only, so choosing one promotes a streamed instrument to RAM as Wavetable does. A project that uses the new modes plays them as silence on firmware 1.4.0 and older and loses the Repitch there (the sample plays at its own length). Every instrument saved before loads and plays unchanged.
9. [+] **MPE out.** Project Settings has a new last row `MPE out` (Off by default, 1 to 15 member channels). When it is on the MIDI instruments play through one MPE lower zone with channel 1 as the master channel. Every sounding note gets its own member channel, so chords and microtonal tunings are tuned per note on an MPE receiver. In a tuning each note is sent as the nearest key plus a pitch bend on its member channel (bend range 48 semitones). The Tracker sends the zone and range messages to the receiver itself when the setting changes, on project load and after a USB reconnect. The `M` step FX bend moves only the note it belongs to. The 16 MIDI channels of the instruments collapse into the zone while it is on. CC, program change and channel pressure go to the master channel. Notes beyond the member count steal the oldest one. Stop releases every member and centres the bends. With Off the MIDI out is byte for byte the old one. A project that does not use it has no `mpe.out` line in `project.mtff`.
10. [+] **MIDI Program Change in.** Config > MIDI has a `Program in` row (Off by default). On, a MIDI Program Change 1 to 16 on channel 1 to 3 over USB or the MIDI In jack picks bank slot 1 to 16 of Synth 1 to 3, the slot the Patch FX uses. On the Synth play page it picks the slot of the side whose channel it arrives on. A slot that holds no patch of the part's engine changes nothing. The Bank column of the Synth play page is redrawn by the page update and not by the audio interrupt, so a burst of Program Changes cannot garble the screen.
11. [+] **Nine more chords for the MIDI Chord FX.** The table grows from 68 to 77 chords with minor add9, add9, 9, m9, maj9, 6/9, 7#9, 7b9 and 7b5. The chord names and the chord capture use them. The Para engines play the nine new chords as Para in 2.0.0 (ACD in the firmware, JU6 1.1.1 and ALCO 1.1.2 in the zip, the 77 chord list in a four note voice with the fifth dropped). A synth engine built for an older firmware keeps its own chord list and plays a chord above its list as plain notes, which holds for a third party engine and for JU6 and ALCO from earlier releases. Firmware 1.4.0 plays the new chord values as plain notes too, and it does not route chords to JU6 1.1.1 and ALCO 1.1.2 at all (it expects a 68 name chord list), so a Para patch plays a chord step there as one plain note per chord note.
12. [+] **Shift + FX1 and Shift + FX2 select FX3 and FX4.** With the 4 FX switch on, Shift + FX1 selects the left FX column of the slots 3/4 (FX3) and Shift + FX2 the right one (FX4), from any column and from either bank, so the button no longer needs a quick second tap. Shift + FX1 again stays on FX3 and never flips back. A plain tap on FX1 or FX2 in the column the cursor is in flips the bank as before and a plain FX1 or FX2 stays in the bank the editor is in. A held Shift + FX button opens no FX list (a plain hold still opens the list). With the switch off, in the Mic/Vol view, in fill and with Pattern, Delete or Copy held, Shift + FX1 and Shift + FX2 select the column as they did. Asked for in tracker-ff issue 2, where a slow tap on FX1 or FX2 opened the FX list instead of flipping the bank. Tracker+ and Tracker Mini build the same pattern editor and the 4 FX switch, so they get the gesture too.
13. [~] **The card module for MOD and IT is now `Plugins/Ext/extras.plg`.** MOD import, IT import and MOD/IT export still load from a card file on first use, but the file is called `extras.plg` and replaces `modit.plg` of 1.4.0. On the original Tracker the same file also carries the custom theme loader (`/Themes/*.json`), the factory self test and the take logic of Capture, so one card file serves all of it. The Tracker+ and the Tracker Mini have all of this in their image and read no `extras.plg`. Without `extras.plg` a custom theme falls back to the built in Original theme and the self test combination shows `Self test needs extras.plg`. The file is bound to the exact image of the release like `modit.plg` was, so copy the one from the same zip. A `modit.plg` from 1.4.0 is no longer read and can be deleted. Without `extras.plg`, with the file of another release or with a damaged one the import and export keys show the same short popup as before and name `extras.plg`. The imported and exported files are byte for byte the same as in 1.4.0.
14. [*] **Line in crackle at a slow tempo (original Tracker).** At a slow tempo the sound of an external synth on the Line in could crackle or break up, mostly after Stop then Play. The Line in sound was taken at whatever moment the audio output got to it. When the output caught up twice in a row, which happened more at a slow tempo and after Stop then Play, it took the same 2.9 ms piece twice and then missed the next one, an audible splice. Every piece is now taken once and in order from a small queue. The price is a fixed delay of 2.9 ms on the Line in sound, the same for the monitor and for recordings. The Tracker+ and the Tracker Mini were not affected. Reported by avemaria666.
15. [*] **Dense MIDI input no longer stalls the audio or resets the unit.** A computer or a sequencer that sends many notes at once over USB or the MIDI In jack with Config > MIDI Notes in on could hold the audio for milliseconds and in the worst case for seconds and a very fast external clock could make the unit reset when it stopped. The unit now takes a limited share of each audio block for incoming USB MIDI and picks up the rest in the next blocks in the same order, so no note is lost and the sound keeps playing (a flood of 16,000 notes a second no longer pushes any audio block past its time). A note that comes in during such a flood can start a block or two late. External clock pulses are played the same way: a pulse that cannot be played in the time of the block waits for the next block, at most four beats of pulses are kept and a Stop drops the pulses that still wait. Clocks up to 999 BPM and ordinary MIDI playing are not affected and the tempo follows the clock as before. MIDI out to the DIN socket no longer waits for the socket when it is busy: a byte that does not fit is dropped and the audio goes on.
16. [*] **More upstream fixes from tracker-ce.** From upstream Polyend Tracker CE, mostly by Łukasz Langa (ambv) with four by Sandroid. Exporting a song or a pattern no longer creates the WAV file again on every buffer flush, so an export no longer depends on the card handing the old clusters back. Exporting the project samples no longer writes past its buffer for a stereo sample and no longer writes every mono sample after it as a stereo file with a doubled left channel. A project, pattern or instrument file that is corrupt or cut short no longer crashes the unit or overruns a buffer when it loads: old-format projects and their samples, the project path, track lengths and zero-length patterns of a pattern file, the sample name of an instrument file, saved synth settings, the wavetable window size and a delay sync rate beyond its table are checked now and a file that ends early finishes loading. The radio of the Sample Recorder keeps the frequency on the channel grid of the region, the frequency bar covers the whole band and tuning or seeking that meets a tuner error stops cleanly. The WAV preview no longer uses the heap in the audio interrupt and gives back its audio blocks cleanly, the autosave stops before another operation takes its place and no longer waits forever when a sample save fails, a failed sample import leaves no gap in the sample memory, the export progress no longer reads past the last instrument and the file list sorts with a strict order. In the Sample Editor the cursor no longer misbehaves on a long release tail in the playback view and the effect preview applies the tune offset of a resampled sample. In Performance mode, ending a Sample start or Sample end override while a Position FX sounds on a looping sample no longer sets the end point to 0 and cuts the sound off, the end point of the instrument is kept. Thank you.
17. [*] **USB audio from the computer no longer breaks up (original Tracker).** The stream from the computer to the unit was adaptive, so the unit repeated or dropped one frame whenever the buffer drifted, about 15 times a second at the measured codec clock, which showed as a tick in a recording of the input. The audio out endpoint is now asynchronous with a feedback endpoint (endpoint 11, 3 bytes, every 32 ms) and the unit tells the computer every audio block how many frames it wants, so a 10 kHz test tone recorded for 60 seconds has no break (14.4 a second before). Frame repeat and skip stay in as a safety net only. A USB bus reset and a Set Configuration from the computer set the audio alternate settings back to 0, so a host never reads a stale setting after a re-plug. In Audio mode 8+3 mono the 11 channel stream leaves no room on the full speed bus for the stream from the computer, so the unit does not offer the audio input interface there (its descriptors are those of a build without it), the source list of the Sample Recorder ends before USB and a USB source stored in the project loads as Line in. Stereo keeps the USB source.
18. [*] **The USB drive shows as Polyend Tracker SD card (original Tracker).** The drive answered the computer with the vendor name of the code it was built from. It reports the vendor Polyend and the product Tracker SD card now.
19. [*] **Project Settings value column (all three trackers).** On the Project Settings page (File screen, F7) the value next to a row of the list comes from one shared text, so the rows that show a text of their own all read the one drawn last. With a Scala file picked on the Scale file row, the Stereo pairs row (original Tracker) read the name of the file instead of how many pairs are linked, and it stayed so after a restart because the page builds it from the project again. A tuning such as 19 EDO did the same to a Scale snap row that showed `3 tracks`, on the Tracker+ and the Tracker Mini as well. Only the text was wrong: the linked pairs, the Scale snap modes and the tuning in the project were never changed and a project saved by 2.0.0 beta 1 needs no repair. Every row has its own text now.
20. [!] **A held note does not follow a tuning change.** The tuning is read when a note starts. A note that is held while you change the tuning (a MIDI key held down, a looping sample) keeps its pitch with no click and the next note takes the new tuning. A change is refused while the sequencer plays, so only a held key can show it.
21. [!] **Tuning on firmware 1.4.0.** Firmware 1.4.0 does not know the tuning lines of `project.mtff` (`tune.n`, `tune.period`, `tune.root`, `tune.hz`, `tune.file`, `tune.cents` and `tune.names`) and the `mpe.out` line. It ignores them, plays the project in 12 notes with the plain MIDI out and writes the lines back unchanged when it saves, so the tuning is still there when the project opens in 2.0.0 again.
22. [~] **One Project Settings page.** Until now two screens had the name Project Settings: the Config group of the Polyend firmware and the File screen page of tracker-ff. There is one now, the File screen page (F7). It lists `4 FX Slots`, `Pattern divider`, `Anti-aliasing`, `Limiter mode`, `Sends mode`, `Extra headroom`, `Snap to zero`, `Macro depth`, `Strum volume`, `Random seed`, `Reroll seed`, `Notes scale`, `Root note`, `Notes layout`, `Scale snap`, `Stereo pairs`, `Tuning` and `MPE out`, with the rows that are only on the original Tracker left out on the Tracker+ and the Tracker Mini. Every moved row writes the same project field as before, so a project saved after the same edits is byte for byte the same file as with the old Config rows and still opens on firmware 1.4.0 and on the Polyend firmware. The Config group `Project Settings` is one row now, `Open the page`, that opens the page. `Performance presets` is a Config value, not a project value, so it moved to the end of Config > General. If you know these rows from the Polyend manual or from tracker-ff 1.x, they are now in File > F7 Project Settings.

#### Synth

1. [+] **New note action for synth parts (original Tracker, Tracker+ and Mini for plugin engines).** The Synth page (1/3) has a `New note` block under the patch slot that sets what a synth part does with the note a track is still holding when the next step note starts. `Release` is the default and sounds as before. `Cut` ends the old note in one audio block. `Fade` fades it out in about 120 ms. `Continue` keeps it sounding until the track's newest note ends (a gate end, an Off step, a mute, Stop or Play). The choice is stored for each part in the project, the same field on all three trackers, so a project moves between them unchanged. A project that never sets it opens as `Release` and a project from 1.4.0 loads unchanged. On the Tracker+ and the Mini the block is the column after Delay send and is there only for a part that plays a plugin engine: the stock engines of those two keep `Release`, so the block is hidden for them.
2. [+] **MPE in on the Synth play page.** A ROLI Seaboard RISE 2 or any MPE controller on the lower zone plays with a pitch bend for each note (range 48 semitones, the MPE default), a pressure for each note that moves the volume (the `Press > Vol` row) and the slide (CC74) on the Mod wheel macro. The zone, the member range and the pressure amount are rows of the page and the zone also follows an RPN 6 message from the controller. It works over USB and DIN on the original Tracker. On the Tracker+ and the Mini the page and its bend wheel work for the whole part (2 semitones, with the stock engines and with the plugin engines), and a part that plays a plugin engine takes the bend and the pressure of each note as on the original Tracker (the stock engines there ignore them). With the zone row Off a plain keyboard on channel 1 plays as before. 3. [+] **Part destinations for Synth Automation.** A Synth Automation slot can drive `Part Pan`, `Part Gain`, `Part Reverb` or `Part Delay` of its synth part, the pan, the volume in 0.1 dB steps and the two send levels. 4. [+] **NNA step FX (original Tracker, Tracker+ and Mini for plugin engines).** The FX list has a new `Q - NNA` step FX with the four values of the `New note` block: `Release`, `Continue`, `Cut` and `Fade`. Morph crossfades between the two parts (equal power, 50 by default). Bank A and Bank B step the patch slot of each part while held notes keep sounding. Perform pressed a third time opens the page too (Shift + Perform opens it from every other page, on Performance it keeps flipping the track pages), a fourth press goes back to Performance page 1. The page keeps its state when you leave and come back to the same instrument: Synth A and B, Morph, the banks, Transpose and the selected column. Press a Bank column again to step on to its Delay and Reverb sends (Bank A, Delay A, Reverb A, then Bank A again, the same for B), 0 to 100, the values of the Instrument page. REC on the page records a mono take of the master output as heard into the free sample memory and lands it as a new instrument named `AB_NNN` in the first free slot after the last used one (the take stops by itself when the sample memory is full). PLK plays 4+4 on the page, every other engine 8+8. Needs CHIP 1.1.1 (`chip.plg`, 29,372 B), which plays 16 voices and keeps the sound of 1.1.0. B and Morph are not stored in the project.
3. [+] **ALCO 1.1.1, a new synth plugin.** `alco.plg` has two oscillators and a sub joined by one of 20 algorithms, with one to three unison oscillators and a chord mode that runs oscillators A and B on every chord note. The wave of an oscillator can be a single cycle wave from `/Waves` on the card. The Wave Mix algorithm blends the two. A patch stores the path of the wave as well as its list index (patch field 101), so the wave stays the right one when files are added to or removed from the card. The firmware answers the path lookup through an optional tail of the files extension of the plugin ABI 1.4 (`wave_ref`, `read_path`, `next_path`, section 12 of the plugin SDK guide). Plugins that do not ask are not affected, a patch without the field plays and saves as before and older firmware keeps the field as an unknown field. ALCO plays a MIDI Chord as paraphonic chord notes and follows the Strum ramp depth of the project. It needs firmware 1.4.0 for the wave mode, because 1.3.x has no files extension (not tried on a unit). Like PHZ and PLK it is a proprietary freeware binary (free use with the Polyend Tracker and tracker-ff, unmodified redistribution, the terms are in the `LICENSE.md` that ships with it), not part of the firmware image and not built from this repository. Copy `alco.plg` with its `.plgi` logo to `/Plugins/Synth/`. A part whose engine is not on the card shows Missing and keeps its settings.
4. [~] **A curated wave set in the release zip and ALCO wave files show a name.** The zip carries a flat `/Waves` folder of 143 single cycle waves from AKWF-FREE by Kristoffer Ekstrand (CC0 1.0): the basic shapes first, then a spread over the AKWF categories. The credit is in `/Waves/AKWF-LICENSE.txt`, the full set of 4,358 waves is at https://github.com/KristofferKarlAxelEkstrand/AKWF-FREE. Waves you already have on the card stay. The A File and B File rows of ALCO's wave mode show the file's name (the base name without `.wav`, a name over 20 characters cut in the middle) and never a number: a row with no file shows `Sine`, which is what ALCO plays for it, a stored name that is not on the card shows `? ` and the name. A card with no `.wav` in `/Waves` does not list the two rows at all (not in the parameter list, the macro targets or the automation destinations), ALCO plays its built-in wave. A patch that stores a subfolder path (`AKWF_bw_sin/AKWF_sin_0001.wav`) finds the same file name directly in `/Waves`.
5. [+] **CHIP 1.1.1.** `chip.plg` has room for 16 voices (8 on each side of the A/B pair on the Synth play page) and renders faster. The sound is the same as 1.0.0 to the last bit, so old projects and patches open as before. For a held 16 note chord on the default patch the estimate is about 280 percent of an audio block before the speed work and about 137 percent after it (an estimate within 40 percent, not measured on a unit). A heavy patch still needs more than one block at 16 voices, so the voice limiter trims it. The file is 29,372 B. The A/B page needs this version for 8+8.
6. [+] **JU6 1.1.0: Para chord mode and Strum.** `ju6.plg` has four new parameters at the end of its list: Chord Mode (Poly or Para), Chord, Voicing and Strum. In Para a note plays as the chord that Chord picks (68 chords, 4 voicings), Strum starts the chord notes one after another and they follow the Strum ramp. Poly is the default and sounds exactly as before. A patch saved with the older file loads unchanged. The new file needs firmware 1.3.0 or newer, on 1.2.x the long Chord list is refused and the JU6 slot reads Missing.
7. [+] **CS-Type 1.0.1.** `cstype.plg` sounds the same as 1.0.0 to the last bit and uses less CPU. On the default patch the estimate is about 10 points of an audio block less at 4 voices (about 25 to 15 percent) and about 21 points less at 8 voices (about 50 to 29 percent), an estimate within 40 percent, not measured on a unit. The file grows from 13,420 B to 24,088 B.
8. [*] **Two tracks on the same pitch.** The sequencer numbers the notes it sends to a synth part now and a note off carries the number of its note. Before, a note off of one track ended every sounding voice of that pitch, so two live notes of the same pitch from two tracks ended together. Notes from the pads and from MIDI in still end by pitch and sound as before. Stop still releases everything.
9. [+] **Morph FX (`o`).** A step FX for synth parts that blends between the open patch slot and a target slot of the Patch FX bank, 0 plays the open slot and 100 the target. The target is the slot of a Patch FX in the other FX column of the step or else a per part Morph target (Shift + the encoder on the slot selector of the Synth screen). The blend stays until another Patch or Morph step changes it. Old firmware plays the step without the FX.
10. [+] **Vary and Randomize for synth patches.** F4 on the synth patch editor moves every continuous value of the patch by up to the shown percent (10 by default), at 100 percent every value is drawn anew. Shift + F4 undoes the last Vary, Shift + the encoder sets the amount in steps of 5. The varied patch is an ordinary edited patch, so Save writes it and leaving without Save keeps the old file.
11. [+] **Strum ramp.** A strummed chord (the Strum FX next to a MIDI Chord) now loses a little level per note in time order and an upstroke is lighter than a downstroke. The depth is a setting of the project, 0 to 100 percent, on the Project Settings page (File screen, F7), row `Strum volume`, in steps of 10. 0 is no ramp and sounds as before 1.4.0. A new project starts at 100. A project from an earlier version has no setting and plays with the ramp off until you turn it on.
12. [+] **Strum from a rolled chord.** When record join writes a chord you played as a roll (the notes on average 15 ms or more apart), the other slot of its MIDI Chord FX pair gets a Strum FX with the speed and the direction of the roll. A busy slot keeps the chord and gets no Strum. Notes pressed together write the chord as before.
13. [+] **Macro depth (`macro.mode`).** New project setting on the Project Settings page: Per patch (the default, every patch plays and loads as before), Add or Scale. With Add or Scale a synth patch that comes into the project later (a new patch, the Init after a Model change and a patch loaded from the library) takes that mode of the macro amount. A patch that is already in the project does not change when the setting changes. The setting is kept in the line `macro.mode` of `project.mtff` and written only for Add and Scale, so a project that does not use it has the file it had before.
14. [+] **ACD (built in).** In Para mode a strummed chord follows the Strum ramp depth of the project: the chord notes lose level in time order and an upstroke is lighter than a downstroke. A ramp of 0 sounds as before. Every Poly patch sounds as before too. The engine writes the left channel only and the part path skips the right channel, with the same sound (see VAP for the figures).
15. [+] **VAP (built in).** The sound is the same as before to the last bit and the part costs less CPU: the engine writes the left channel only, the part path skips the right channel and the voice records sit in the faster memory. Measured on the unit (3 runs per side, the 1.4.0 image against the same image without the synth CPU changes): one voice costs 47 us less per audio block (789 to 742 us), four voices 97 us less (1,111 to 1,014 us). These figures include the changes every synth shares, not the mono path alone.
16. [+] **WTFM (built in).** The same changes as VAP: the sound is the same to the last bit, the engine writes the left channel only and the voice records sit in the faster memory. The saving was not measured on the unit for WTFM alone.
17. [!] Firmware 1.2.0 keeps at most 768 bytes of patch data it does not know. A patch saved by 1.3.0 or later that carries more than 765 bytes of such data fails to load on 1.2.0. Normal patches carry 8 to 52 bytes.
18. [!] The first install of a plugin engine can still cost a short audio gap in a busy project.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-13 - tracker-ff 1.3.0 (original Tracker, MK66)

Version 1.3.0 of the tracker-ff firmware for the original Tracker. The Tracker+ and the Mini are not part of it.

### Added

1. **USB audio.** Built in. With USB audio on, the unit shows up as an audio input on a computer after the next start. With it off, the unit presents only the stock USB interfaces.
2. **Glue master insert.** `glue.plg` in `/Plugins/Master/`, a bus compressor with Auto makeup and a Character setting (Clean, Glue, Smooth, Punch or Colour). Its Makeup reads `Auto` at the bottom step. A plugin can name the end values of a numeric parameter now (`PLUG_PARAM_NAME_MIN` and `PLUG_PARAM_NAME_MAX`), older plugins and hosts are not affected. There are eight inserts now. Glue and Phaser do not fit the insert memory together, the second of the two shows `Full`.
3. **Paraphony.** A synth plugin can answer a chord query of the firmware (an optional extension of the plugin format, older plugins and hosts are not affected). ACD is the one engine of this release with a Para mode. It has three new parameters at the end of its Voice section: Chord Mode (Poly or Para), Chord and Strum. The mode is stored per patch. In Para a MIDI Chord step plays its notes as one voice with extra oscillators through the one filter and the envelopes, which costs less audio CPU than a voice per note. Patches from 1.2.x load with Poly and sound as before. Engines without a Para mode play the chord notes as separate voices. The patch Chord plays for notes from the pads, a MIDI keyboard and plain steps, and a MIDI Chord step FX wins for its own step (the same goes for Strum).
4. **Chord capture and chord names.** Play MIDI keys together with the cursor on a MIDI or synth step (any column), or hold pads with the cursor on a MIDI Chord cell, and the Tracker writes the chord: the lowest note becomes the step note and the chord goes into the FX value. A popup at the cursor shows the chord name and the held notes. A chord that is not an exact match gets the nearest one and a `~` in front of its name (`~Cmaj7`). A group with no near match leaves the step as it was. One Undo takes the chord back. The title bar and the popup show chord names with root and bass such as `Fmaj7/C`, the Fill list shows the name without a root such as `maj7 inv2`. MIDI Chord values above 29 (the inversions) play only the bass note on stock firmware, so a project with captured inversions sounds thinner there.
5. **Strum FX.** `u - Strum` in the FX slot next to a `0 - MIDI Chord` starts the chord notes one after another: 1 to 63 up (the lowest note first), 65 to 127 down, 2 ms between two notes per unit, 0 and 64 off. The notes start on audio blocks of about 2.9 ms, so `+10` is about 20 ms. A Para ACD patch strums inside its voice with its own Strum parameter.
6. **Record join.** Notes you play together while recording are written as one step with a MIDI Chord. With several tracks armed the chord joins onto the track under the cursor.
7. **Synth order.** Shift + F1 on the Model column of the Synth screen edits the engine list: Shift + Up and Down move an engine, Delete hides it, Insert shows it again, and the arrow keys wrap from the last engine to the first. The order is saved in `/Plugins/synth-order.txt`. Projects and `.patch` files still store the engine itself, so nothing in them changes. An engine that the open project plays is shown at the top even when hidden, and an engine without a line in the file is shown at the end.
8. **Macro depth Scale.** A new patch scales a macro amount to the room between the parameter value and its end, so an Amount of 1.00 reaches the maximum and -1.00 the minimum (the Amount is shown from -2.00 to 2.00, 1.00 is 100 %). Patches from 1.2.x keep the old rule. Firmware 1.2.x plays every macro with the old rule, so a 1.3.0 patch that uses a macro amount sounds different there.
9. **Features page.** Config > Features lists the options of the firmware that have a switch of their own. Each one has a State row and a page with its settings, and the settings are kept per feature in `/Features/` on the card. Reset config clears that folder. A feature that needs a restart says so and offers Restart now. The page lists USB audio and Chord names. Chord names switches the MIDI Chord cells of the pattern grid between short names such as `m7` and the interval digits.
10. **Note-on admission.** A synth note whose estimated cost would push the audio block over its time budget (87 % of the block) starts at the start of the next block, at most one block late and never dropped. A voice that a new note takes over in a full pool fades out in the block of the note, and a released voice that is near silence is freed at once.

Items 3 to 6 and 8 (paraphony, chord capture and chord names, Strum, record join and macro depth) are explained in the user guide [`docs/chords_and_macros.md`](docs/chords_and_macros.md). Item 7 (Synth order) is explained in the user guide [`docs/synth_order.md`](docs/synth_order.md).

### Changed

1. **Sample input chain idles.** The USB input stream of the original sends no data, and a gate whose pre-FX mixer inputs are silenced builds no output block, so an unused input chain costs less CPU. The output is unchanged.
2. **CHIP is an installable engine.** CHIP is no longer built into the image. It is `chip.plg` in `/Plugins/Synth/` like the other plugin engines, and it frees about 15 KB of flash. The sound, the parameters and the numbers are unchanged, so old projects and `.patch` files open on it as before. A part on CHIP shows `Missing: CHIP` on a card without the plugin. When you update from 1.2.x, copy `chip.plg` to `/Plugins/Synth/` too.
3. **Patch FX.** `O - Patch` now applies the macros and the automation of the slot it switches to, together with its values. An LFO or envelope that sits on the same slot type in both patches keeps running, one that changes type starts new and an automation slot that is Off reads zero.
4. **Model list rest.** Every step onto another engine in the Model list now restarts the 1 s rest, built-in engines included. Before, only a step onto an engine that was not installed waited, so scrolling with the arrows, their key repeat or the encoder picked each built-in engine on the way.
5. **Transient Shaper.** The sample editor effect has new envelope times, and its Sustain gain is held back while a hit is rising.
6. **Add to Empty Slot.** With files selected in the Sample Loader, Shift with the add button adds the selection and does not jump to the first empty slot. After a jump the instrument list and the memory bar refresh.
7. **Short delay.** The delay runs only the lines of the mode that is on (mono or ping pong), which uses less audio CPU. The sound is unchanged while the mode stays. Switching the mode with echoes ringing now crossfades over about 90 ms and carries the echoes over, where it used to cut to unrelated echoes with a click.
8. **Master insert 2 runs from the shared SRAM slot.** Both master inserts run from one 8 KB internal SRAM slot, insert 1 from the bottom and insert 2 from the top, so insert 2 no longer writes to the data flash. A plugin that fits alone but not beside the other insert shows `Full:<name>` and loads when the other one leaves room. The synth plugin bank takes the two freed sectors and now has all 32 sectors of the data flash.
9. **Lower audio CPU beyond the delay.** The reverb loop runs at half rate with the same room size, damping and decay time, and its lines fit internal SRAM, so the 32 KB SDRAM buffer is gone. The limiter keeps its state in registers and divides only above the threshold. A sounding synth part skips the parameter refresh when nothing changed. The synth sends are skipped when both pre-FX send gains are 0. The sample voice loops keep their state in registers. The internal sequencer clock counts in integers. All of these give the same output bit for bit.

### Fixed

1. Installing a synth plugin into the data flash no longer makes the audio crackle. The audio output renders ahead into a deeper buffer while the flash erases, and the erase works in short slices.
2. The delay feedback and the reverb damping are set the same way at the start as a project load sets them. Before, they started from the raw project values and the first project load corrected them.
3. The master inserts of a project load now wait like the synth engines do: 1 s after the load finished, then one insert at a time, 250 ms apart. Before, both read the card in the first main loop pass after the load. A pick on the Inserts page still loads after 400 ms.
4. A part whose engine is not on the card (for example CHIP without `chip.plg`) keeps that engine. The encoder, the arrow keys and F1 on its Model list show the `not installed` popup and change nothing, and no row of the list is highlighted. Before, one encoder click turned the part into ACD, and the list highlighted ACD so that Delete in the edit mode hid ACD.

### Known issues

1. A patch saved by 1.3.0 with a macro amount in Scale mode plays with the old Add rule on firmware 1.2.x, so that macro sounds different there.
2. Macro depth mode is stored per patch and the unit has no row to switch it yet. New patches are Scale, patches without the mode stored are Add.
3. Glue and Phaser do not fit the insert memory together. The second of the two shows `Full` and passes the audio.
4. A part whose engine is missing still opens its Macros and Automation pages, and edits made there are not kept.
5. Firmware 1.2.0 keeps at most 768 bytes of patch data it does not know. A patch saved by 1.3.0 that carries more than 765 bytes of such data fails to load on 1.2.0. Normal patches carry 8 to 52 bytes.
6. The first install of a plugin engine can still cost a short audio gap in a busy project. The gap is much shorter than in 1.2.0.
7. Shift + Power screenshots can freeze the Tracker (known since 1.2.0). Hold the power button to switch it off and on again.

Updating from 1.1.0 or 1.2.0: see [`UPGRADING.md`](UPGRADING.md). `chip.plg` has to be on the card for projects that use CHIP.

5. The CHIP engine no longer overloads the audio interrupt with many voices.
6. Changing the engine of a synth part, loading a project, saving a recording, listing a folder and opening the value list of a synth parameter no longer write through a null pointer when the heap is short. Large synth blocks come from the external memory and a failed allocation ends the action with an error.
7. The synth patch editor refuses a parameter or macro index outside the patch.
8. Power off always switches the Tracker off. Before, it could hang at the end with the screen on and every key dead. The wait for a running card operation is now bounded, a short heap no longer faults while the shutdown bar is created and a software deadline switches the Tracker off if the sequence stalls.

## [1.0.0] - Initial Community Edition Release

> **⚠️ Important :: Project Compatibility Notice:**
> To support dedicated per-step **Micro-move (`m`)** and **Volume (`V`)** columns alongside **`Fx1`** and **`Fx2`**, pattern files (`.mtp`) have been updated to a new format (**Version 6**).
> 
> - **Loading old projects works automatically**: Existing projects created on official/older firmwares load seamlessly and are upgraded in memory.
>   
> - **Saving updates the project to Version 6 (not backward-compatible with older firmware)**: Once you save a project on Community Edition firmware, its saved `.mtp` pattern files cannot be read by older firmwares. **Please make a backup of your `Projects/` folder on your SD card or use `Save As` to save under a new project name, if you ever plan to roll back to an older firmware.**

### Added

1. **Open Synth SDK & New `CHIP` + `DIRT` Synthesizer Engines (Tracker+ & Tracker Mini)**
   * Added a new Open Synth SDK allowing the community to add new synthesizer engines. `CHIP` is the first implementation of this open architecture.
   * Added **`DIRT`** (wave-shaping/distortion synth).
   * See [`docs/custom_synth_sdk.md`](docs/custom_synth_sdk.md) to learn more about developing your own engines.

2. **Modular Sample Editor Effect & Generator Plugins (All 3 Devices)**
   * Added hot-swappable DSP plugins loaded from `/Effects/*.ptfx` on the SD card. 
   * Supports up to **12 parameters** (across 3 pages) and up to **128 installed plugins**.
   * Added the complete `.ptfx` SDK, `ptfx_builder.py`, and 6 reference effects/generators (`Add Silence`, `Analog Kick Gen`, `Chiptune Voice Gen`, `Multi-Mode Filter`, `Stereo Delay`, `Stereo to Mono`) in [`editor-fx-sdk/`](editor-fx-sdk/).
   * See [`docs/modular_effects_system.md`](docs/modular_effects_system.md) to learn more about developing your own plugins.

3. **Wavetable Envelopes (`WT Env`), Wavetable LFOs (`WT LFO`) & Step FX (`w` / `y`) (All 3 Devices)**
   * Added `WT Env` and `WT LFO` modulation types across all 7 instrument parameters (`Volume`, `Panning`, `Cutoff`, `Wavetable Position`, `Granular Position`, `Granular Length`, `Finetune`) with **128 Factory (`F 001`-`F 128`)** and **128 User (`U 001`-`U 128`)** morphable contours and dual-axis `Warp X/Y` shaping.
   * Load your own wavetables to overwrite the 128 User shapes, by uploading them to `/Envelopes/*.wav`
   * Added `w` (WT Volume Env) and `y` (WT Filter Env) pattern step FX for per-step envelope duration/warp/ADSR shaping.
   * See [`docs/wavetable_envelopes.md`](docs/wavetable_envelopes.md).

4. **Custom Color Themes & Font Packages (All 3 Devices)**
   * You can now create your own themes and load them from `/Themes/*.json` on your SD Card (up to 29 custom themes alongside the 3 built-in themes) and custom antialiased font packages from `/Fonts/*.ptfont` (up to 30 custom fonts) selectable in `Config > General > Theme / Font`.
   * Create your own with the [Web Theme & Font Editor](https://polyend.github.io/tracker-theme-editor/) ([`polyend/tracker-theme-editor`](https://github.com/polyend/tracker-theme-editor)). For more information see: [`docs/custom_themes_and_fonts.md`](docs/custom_themes_and_fonts.md).

5. **Performance Mode Enhancement (All 3 Devices)**
   * Added page toggling between Sample Tracks and MIDI/Synth Tracks in Performance Mode. Just keep pressing `[Perform]` to toggle between the views.
   * Oh, for OG Tracker users, there's a small surprise that goes along well with this change.

6. **Hybrid SD Card Sample Streaming (All 3 Devices)**
   * Long samples  (`256 KB` and larger) now keep only their initial attack in sample memory while streaming the rest of the audio directly from the SD card in the background. This lets you load a massive amount of stems, loops, and recordings into a single project while using only a fraction of your sample RAM.
     
   * **What Streams on Each Device**:
     * **Tracker+ & Tracker Mini**: Streams **`One-Shot`**, **`Forward Loop`**, **`Backward Loop`**, **`Ping-Pong Loop`**, **`Slice`**, and **`Beat Slice`** instruments (`Wavetable` and `Granular` instruments remain fully in RAM).
       
     * **Original Tracker**: Streams **`One-Shot`**, **`Forward Loop`**, **`Backward Loop`**, and **`Ping-Pong Loop`** instruments, while keeping **`Slice`** and **`Beat Slice`** instruments (alongside `Wavetable` and `Granular`) fully in RAM to keep performance solid across multiple tracks.
       
   * **Works Transparently with the Sample Editor**: You can still edit streamed samples in the Sample Editor or switch play modes at any time. The Tracker automatically loads the full sample into RAM while you edit it and returns to streaming once your edits are applied. This excludes very long sample however, due to RAM restrictions. If you plan to stream multi-minutes samples, please prepare them outside of the device.
   * See [`docs/developer_guide.md`](docs/developer_guide.md#3-copy-on-write-workspaceinstruments-incremental-project-saver--hybrid-sd-streaming) for technical details.

7. **`Slice` & `Beat Slice` Instruments can now `Play Thru` (All 3 Devices)**
   * Added a per-instrument **`Play Thru`** (`Off` / `On`) setting on the **`More`** page of the **Sample Playback** screen for both **`Slice`** and **`Beat Slice`** modes.
   * When **`Play Thru`** is **`On`**, triggering any slice starts at that slice marker and continues playing through subsequent slices until the note is released (`Note Off` / `CUT` / `FAD`) or the end of the sample is reached.

8. **Dedicated Micro-move (`m`) & Volume (`V`) Step View (All 3 Devices)**
   * Each pattern step now stores dedicated **Micro-move (`m`)** (`0..100`) and **Volume / Velocity (`V`)** (`0..100`) values in addition to its two custom step FX slots (**`Fx1`** and **`Fx2`**), freeing both FX slots for creative use.
   * Pressing **`[Note]`** in Pattern View while the cursor is already on the **`Note`** column toggles the two effect columns between the standard **`[Fx1 | Fx2]`** view and the new **`[m | V]`** view.
   * Unquantized live recording (**Record Micro-move**) and velocity recording (**Record Velocity**) now write directly into the dedicated **`m`** and **`V`** step fields instead of occupying **`Fx1`** and **`Fx2`**.
   * Full support in **`[m | V]`** view for Jog-wheel / `±` editing, Pad entry, `Delete`, `Copy` / `Paste`, `Invert`, `Undo` / `Redo`, and the **`Fill`** tool (`Constant`, `From-To`, `Random`, `Euclidean`).

9. **Granular Length Automation & Step FX (`z`) (All 3 Devices)**
   * Added **`Granular Length`** as a modulatable parameter in **Instrument Automation**, allowing you to animate grain size over time using an Envelope, LFO and the new Wavetable Envelope or LFOs.
   * Added the **`z`** step FX (`0`-`100%`) to control grain length on a per-step basis (`0-50%` gives fine `1.0-100.0 ms` control for tight micro-grains, while `50-100%` sweeps `100.0-1000.0 ms` for lush clouds).
     
10. **More Chance Conditions (All 3 Devices)**
   * Spiced up the **`Chance` (`C`)** step FX beyond percentage probability (`C  0`-`C100`) with conditional and cycle-based modes inspired by the Polyend Play:
     * **Group with Last (`C GL`)**: Plays only if the previous conditional step evaluated on the same track played.
     * **Not Last (`C !L`)**: Plays only if the previous conditional step evaluated on the same track was skipped.
     * **Play N / Skip M (`C1:1`-`C6:6`)**: Plays the step for `N` consecutive pattern playthroughs, then skips it for `M` playthroughs (`N, M` in `1..6`).
     * **Skip N / Play M (`CS11`-`CS66`)**: Skips the step for `N` consecutive pattern playthroughs, then plays it for `M` playthroughs (`N, M` in `1..6`).
     
11. **Reactivated Multi-Folder SD Card Image Viewer (All 3 Devices)**
   * Reactivated and expanded the JPEG image viewer under `Config > Info` with three user folders (`Reference`, `Notes` and `Custom` in `/Images/<Folder>/1.jpg` … `48.jpg`), supporting Left/Right, Jog-wheel, and direct Pad `1-48` selection.
   * See [`docs/image_viewer.md`](docs/image_viewer.md).

### Changed & Fixed

 * **Live Recording Improvements (All 3 Devices)**:
	* You can now play much tigther notes on single-armed tracks. As in: If you play a second note before releasing the first, it won't get lost.
	* Audible first note after preroll, and configurable first-note recording timing threshold (if you tend to play early).
	  
* **Exact `44100 Hz` Offline Audio Export (All 3 Devices)**:
	* Fixed sample rate drift during offline master and stem export so rendered `.wav` files match real-time `44100 Hz` playback pitch and timing.
	* Also removed excessive silence at end of renders.
	  
* **Removed clicks on rendered samples and editor effects (All 3 devices)**
	* No more clicks at the end of your samples.
	  
* **MIDI Overhaul (All 3 devices)**
	* Fixed USB MIDI enumeration for Windows 11 (March 2026 update).
	* Fixed long-term tempo drift and overhauled how MIDI Clock is sent out. Timing is now ~15× tighter and stays locked over hours of playback.
	* Fixed an issue where the Tracker could start slightly late (by one clock tick) when pressing Play on an external master clock or DAW. Step 1 now fires dead-on the downbeat.
	* The Tracker now responds to MIDI `Continue` and `Song Position Pointer` messages.
	* Improved how the Tracker follows incoming MIDI Clock when your computer or master device sends slightly uneven clock bursts, and fixed the `Clock Sync Delay` setting so fine-tuning your setup's latency works predictably.
	* Fixed low-level USB and TRS MIDI buffer issues that could occasionally drop clocks, notes, or CC automation during busy patterns.
	
* **Faster Project Loading & Saving (All 3 Devices)**:
	* Opening a project no longer duplicates all of its audio samples into a temporary folder on the SD card before loading them into memory. Instead, samples are loaded directly from your project folder, making even large, sample-heavy projects open in a fraction of the time while drastically reducing SD card wear.
	* Saving changes to your current project now only writes out samples whose audio was actually edited or newly added, while pattern edits and instrument parameter tweaks save almost instantly. (`Save As` still saves a complete, standalone copy of your project and all its samples).
	  
* **Major Internal Memory, SDRAM & Audio Engine Optimization (All 3 Devices)**:
	* Reclaimed internal SRAM and SDRAM across all three devices to power Hybrid SD Card Sample Streaming while keeping 100% of the original sample RAM pool intact (`11.25 MB` on Original Tracker / `60 MB` on Tracker+ & Tracker Mini), leaving generous internal RAM headroom (`~128 KB` free on Original Tracker, `~22.5 KB` `SRAM_ITC` + `~21.5 KB` `SRAM_DTC` + `~9.3 KB` `SRAM_OC` on Tracker+ & Tracker Mini) and up to `~29 MB` (`IMX`) / `~5.3 MB` (`MK66`) of reclaimable SDRAM for future community ideas.
	* Optimized real-time audio rendering for cleaner playback on heavy multi-track projects.
	  
* **Open-Source Build Consolidation (All 3 Devices)**:
	* Unified `core/`, `platforms/mk66/`, `platforms/imx/`, `synth/`, `util/`, and `editor-fx-sdk/` into a single monorepo built via a top-level [`Makefile`](Makefile) and [`version.mk`](version.mk).
	* Removed the proprietary NES emulator and repurposed its `EXTERNAL_RAM` region (`externalRamScratchBuff`, `768 KB`) as the shared scratch pool for `.ptfx` plugins, offline audio export, SD `.wav` preview, and modal file browsers. (Sorry gamers).
