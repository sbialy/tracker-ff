# Changelog

All notable changes to the **Polyend Tracker Community Edition (CE)** firmware across **Original Tracker** (`MK66`), **Tracker+** and **Tracker Mini** (`IMX`) are documented in this file.

---

## [1.4.0] - 2026-11-18 - tracker-ff 1.4.0 (original Tracker, MK66)

Marks: [+] new, [~] changed, [*] fixed, [!] known issue.

Updating from 1.3.x: see [`UPGRADING.md`](UPGRADING.md). New files go on the card (`Effects/se_*.ptfx`, `Plugins/Ext/modit.plg` and `alco.plg`).

Version 1.4.0 of the tracker-ff firmware for the original Tracker. The Tracker+ and the Mini are not part of it.

### Tracker

1. [+] **FX3 and FX4.** `4 FX Slots` on the Project Settings page gives every pattern step two more FX slots (off by default and not CE compatible) and the FX buttons flip the pattern editor between the slots 1/2 and 3/4 (Details, Tracker 1 and 2).
2. [+] **USB audio 8+3.** Audio mode 8+3 mono in Config > USB sends tracks 1 to 8 and synth parts 1 to 3 to the computer as 11 separate inputs (Details, Tracker 3 and 4).
3. [+] **USB drive.** With the feature on in Config > Features, Config > USB > Storage shows the SD card on the computer as a USB drive (Details, Tracker 5).
4. [+] **Parts.** The Song screen (Shift + F8) gets a Parts view that builds, plays and edits Parts (a source pattern for each track plus a length and repeats) and an edit is heard while a Part plays (Details, Tracker 7 to 12).
5. [+] **Chance FX (`K`).** A failed condition skips only the other FX of the step and the long pass conditions `1/8` to `8/8` and `P01` to `P16` work in Chance and Chance FX (Details, Tracker 20 and 21).
6. [+] **Snap FX (`N`).** It sets the scale snap of a track (Off, Nearest, Down or Up) from its step on and the new `Scale snap` row of the Project Settings page sets it for each track (Details, Tracker 22 and 27).
7. [+] **Tape (`W` and `Z`).** It slows a track to a stop (`W`) or spins a note up from a standstill (`Z`) on sample and Poly synth tracks (Details, Tracker 23).
8. [+] **Pattern length for each track.** Each track can have its own length, set with Shift + the encoder while Length (F2) is held and shown as `3/8` (Details, Tracker 25).
9. [+] **Follow chord.** Page 3 of the pattern editor (More twice) has Follow (F4) and Chord (F5) to play the notes of a track snapped or transposed to the chord track (Details, Tracker 26).
10. [+] **Random seed.** Set on the Project Settings page it makes the random parts of the sequencer play the same take on every Play from the same position (Details, Tracker 28).
11. [+] **Send ducking.** A chosen source pulls the delay and reverb returns down while it sounds (Master > Limiter, page F6) (Details, Tracker 30).
12. [+] **Project Settings page and `project.mtff`.** File screen F7 opens one page for the settings of the project and the new file next to `project.mt` stores those that the `.mt` has no room for (Details, Tracker 17 and 18).
13. [*] **Performance overwrote a pattern.** Leaving Performance mode while stopped no longer lets a pattern save write the switched out tracks over the pattern (Details, Tracker 6).
14. [!] **FX3 and FX4 on older firmware.** A project that uses them is silent in those two slots on firmware 1.3.x and on stock CE and loses their content when it is saved there with Save As (Details, Tracker 41).

### Synth

1. [+] **Synth play page and A/B.** Shift + Perform opens a page that plays one synth part from a keyboard or a DAW on USB or DIN and A/B adds a second part on the same keys with a Morph crossfade and up to 8 voices on each side (Details, Synth 1 and 2).
2. [+] **ALCO 1.1.1.** `alco.plg` is a new synth plugin with two oscillators and a sub joined by one of 20 algorithms and single cycle waves from `/Waves` (Details, Synth 3 and 4).
3. [+] **Engine updates.** CHIP 1.1.1 renders faster with the same sound. JU6 1.1.0 gets a Para chord mode with Strum. CS-Type 1.0.1 uses less CPU with the same sound (Details, Synth 5 to 7).
4. [+] **Morph FX (`o`).** It blends a synth part between its open patch slot and a target slot of the Patch FX bank (Details, Synth 9).
5. [+] **Vary and Randomize.** F4 on the synth patch editor moves every continuous value of the patch by up to a set percent (Details, Synth 10).
6. [+] **Strum ramp.** A strummed chord loses level note by note by the depth set in the project and a chord you play as a roll is recorded with a Strum FX (Details, Synth 11 and 12).
7. [~] **ACD, VAP and WTFM.** These built-in engines sound the same as before to the last bit and use less CPU (Details, Synth 14 to 16).

### Details

#### Tracker

1. [+] **FX3 and FX4 (a switch per project, not CE compatible).** The row `4 FX Slots` of the Project Settings page (File screen, F7) gives every pattern step two more FX slots. They are kept in a file next to each pattern (`pattern_NN.mtx`) and the project says so in the line `fx.slots 4` of `project.mtff`. Off is the default for new projects and for every project of an earlier version and a project with the switch off writes exactly the files it wrote before. The pattern files stay as they are. **A project that uses FX3 and FX4 is silent in those two slots on firmware 1.3.x and on stock CE and it loses their content when it is saved there with Save As** (only the `.mtp` files are copied). When another firmware changes a pattern file the saved FX3 and FX4 of that pattern are not applied. They stay on the card and the status line of `/Plugins/host.txt` says so. Turning the switch off keeps the files, stops playing them and shows a warning. Turning it on again brings them back. The switch can only be changed while the sequencer is stopped. A pattern holds 256 extra FX at most. MOD and IT export write two FX slots, so FX3 and FX4 are not exported.
2. [+] **Pattern editor: FX3 and FX4, V and m always visible.** With the 4 FX switch on, a tap on the FX1 or FX2 button while the cursor is already in that column flips the two FX columns between the slots 1/2 and 3/4. Bank 3/4 is green and the title bar says `FX3:` or `FX4:`. On the same parameter FX2 beats FX1, FX1 beats FX4 and FX4 beats FX3. Chance FX, Random value, Strum, Morph and Arp with Chord look only at the other slot of their own pair. The volume and micro-move of a step show as a small bar and tick in the cell and as `V100 m12` in the title bar. Config > Pattern V/m columns gives them columns of their own with three tracks on screen. In edit mode the third page of the buttons under the screen (More twice) has F6 `V/m FX to fields` to move the V and m FX of steps with a note into the step fields by hand. It is not bit exact when the groove is on, so it never runs by itself. Paste and invert in the Mic/Vol view acted on the opposite column of the held button, which is fixed.
3. [+] **USB audio 8+3 mono.** Config > USB has an Audio mode row (the USB entry shows while USB audio or USB drive is on in Config > Features and is hidden when both are off): Stereo (the master mix, as before) or 8+3 mono, 11 inputs on the computer: tracks 1 to 8 dry before pan (after volume and mute), then synth parts 1 to 3 as mono sums, lined up as the Tracker mixes them. No master, reverb, delay or inserts on these channels. The mode takes effect at the next start (the row reads Restart until then).
4. [+] **USB audio 8+3 auto mute.** While the computer records the 8+3 stream, the master Delay and Reverb sleep to save CPU. It engages after one second of streaming and releases one second after the stream stops, so a stream that macOS keeps open does not toggle it. Meanwhile the Mute keys of the Delay and the Reverb are locked and read `8+3` on Master > Global Mixer 3/3. Your mute state is never changed or saved and comes back as you left it and a project saved during streaming keeps it. The 8+3 channels are tapped before the sends, so the recording does not change. Config > USB > FX in 8+3 (Auto mute by default, Off) turns it off at once. Stereo mode never mutes and a song export renders with the effects as always.
5. [+] **USB drive.** Config > Features > USB drive (Off by default, takes effect at the next start) adds a mass storage interface to the USB descriptors. With it on, Config > USB > Storage shows the SD card on the computer as a USB drive (Drive mode). The power button does nothing until you press Exit and Exit reads `/Workspace` back, so what the computer wrote there wins over the project in RAM. Back up the card before the first use and eject the drive on the computer before Exit. With the feature Off the unit enumerates exactly as before and it works together with USB audio.
6. [*] **Performance overwrote a pattern.** Leaving Performance mode while stopped now loads the pattern back and a mask keeps the tracks that Performance switched out of every pattern save. Before, a pattern save in that state could write the switched tracks over the pattern.
7. [+] **Parts.** Shift + F8 on the Song screen switches between the Patterns and the Parts view. A Part is a source pattern for each track plus a length and a number of repeats, up to 255 Parts. Items 8 to 12 of Details, Tracker describe how to build, play and edit them.
8. [+] **Parts, arrange and build.** The Parts view is a table: Parts as rows, the 16 tracks as columns, a Length column, a Repeats column and a song map of the whole list. The title bar names the mode (`Parts - ARRANGE` or `Parts - BUILD`). With Rec off the F row plays: F1 Play Part (loops it, while playing Loop Part or Continue Song), F2 Play Song from the selected Part (while playing it queues the selected Part, Shift queues it for the end of the pass, on the playing or queued Part it stops), F3 Go to Start (Shift: the Part that played last), F4 Go to End, F5 Bake, F6 Loop Song (On or Off, Play Song stops after the last Part unless it is On), F8 Tempo. With Rec on the F row builds: F1 to F8 are the track buttons of the half the cursor is in (1 to 8 or 9 to 16), shown by a red rule under the title bar, a band behind those 8 columns and the line `F1-F8 = tracks 1-8`. Hold a track button and press pad N to give that track pattern N, hold it and turn the encoder for the pattern plus or minus 1, tap it alone to turn the track Off and back on. Shift + pad 1 to 6 picks the pad bank (patterns 1 to 48 up to 241 to 255). Left and Right move the cursor track and, past track 16, onto the Length and the Repeats field (the encoder changes the field there, Left goes back), Up, Down and the encoder move the Part, Shift + Up or Down selects a range. Shift + encoder sets the length one step per click (Auto, then 1 to 128 steps, shown as `3b+4` for 3 bars and 4 steps), Shift + pad 11 to 19 sets it to Auto or 1 to 8 whole bars and Shift + Left or Right the repeats (End, then 1 to 64), three separate controls. Hold a track button and press Shift + the encoder or Shift + pad 11 to 19 to give that track a start step into its pattern (the cell reads `4:17`, the line under the table says `Track 1: from step 17`): the track plays the pattern from that step to its end and a longer Part tiles that part only. Shift + Copy is Unlink: it copies the window of the track under the cursor (start and length applied) into the next free pattern and points the cell to it. Copy copies the selected Parts, Insert pastes after, Shift + Insert pastes before (with nothing copied Insert duplicates the selected Part, in an empty list it makes Part 1 from the pattern on the screen). Delete turns cells Off and Shift + Delete removes Parts. The pad LEDs light the patterns of the Part under the cursor. The header shows the queued Part as `Part 3 > 6`.
9. [+] **Shift + Song.** Shift + Song opens the Song screen in the Parts view from the Pattern screen. Shift + Song there switches back, as Shift + F8 does.
10. [+] **Edit patterns while a Part plays.** Rec on the Pattern screen works during Play Part, Loop Part and Play Song. The edit is heard at once in the playing pass and is written back to the source pattern files about 300 ms after the last edit, at the end of the Part, at Stop and on every project save. Every Part that uses that source on that track plays the edit too. Performance mode stays refused while a Part plays, with a timed popup.
11. [+] **Performance Insert.** In Performance mode Insert saves what Performance plays now (the pattern of each track and the muted tracks) as a new Part after the selected one (a track with no notes is Off). The title bar shows `Saved as Part N` for 3 seconds and the Parts view tints the new Part.
12. [+] **Parts storage.** The Parts list stays in `project.mtff` and `project.mt` and the pattern files are not touched. A firmware without Parts plays the song playlist and keeps the Part lines. The start steps are in `pat.start.N` lines that are written only for a Part that has one, a firmware without them keeps those lines and plays the whole pattern. `project.mtff` can be up to 32 KB now (it was 20 KB).
13. [*] **Song grid with 8 or more Parts.** The stock Song grid drew every Part into one display list slot that holds 7 rows, so 8 Parts gave a black screen and 12 Parts overran the next slot. Every drawing stage is bounded to its slot now and the Parts view draws its own table, which stays under the 2,043 word frame limit (1,926 words at 12 Parts, at most 1,922 up to 40).
14. [*] **Upstream fixes from tracker-ce.** 54 fixes from the tracker-ce pull requests #87, #90 and #91 to #99, 52 by Łukasz Langa (ambv) and two by Emanuele Lapponi. They cover the analog kick click at the end of its decay, pattern and playlist stops at the end of a pattern or a full song, MIDI Start on the Song screen, the range checks of the patch editor, instrument, slice and envelope bounds, the Sample Editor zoom, the Sample Recorder, the Sample Loader, sample import, Delete unused, the file manager paths and the File browser, Performance mode, the fill popup and the pattern cursor wrap. Thank you.
15. [~] **MOD import, IT import and MOD/IT export need `modit.plg` on the card.** The three importers and exporters moved out of the firmware image into a card module, `/Plugins/Ext/modit.plg`, which frees 12,288 bytes of flash (three sectors). The module is bound to the exact image it was built with, so copy the one from the same release zip (the zip and the card kit carry it). It loads on the first use (the first MOD or IT import, the first export), which takes a moment and the file stays in the slot until the next start. Without it, with a file of another release or with a damaged one, the import and export keys show a short popup (`MOD/IT module missing`, `modit.plg is for another firmware`, `modit.plg is damaged`) and nothing else changes. The files written and read are byte for byte the same as before. FX3 and FX4 are still not exported, MOD and IT write two FX slots.
16. [~] **Nine Sample Editor effects moved to the card.** Amplifier, Overdrive, Delay, Bitcrusher, Chorus, Flanger, Equalizer, Limiter and Compressor are no longer in the firmware image. They are `.ptfx` files in `/Effects/` (`se_amplifier.ptfx`, `se_overdrive.ptfx`, `se_delay.ptfx`, `se_bitcrusher.ptfx`, `se_chorus.ptfx`, `se_flanger.ptfx`, `se_equalizer.ptfx`, `se_limiter.ptfx`, `se_compressor.ptfx`), they keep their place in the effect list of the Sample Editor and render bit for bit what the built-in ones rendered (checked on 2,163 cases against the built-in code). Without a file the row reads `Missing: <name>` and the effect cannot be applied, like a missing synth engine. The image is 7,364 B shorter. Normalizer, Crop, Reverse, Fade In, Fade Out, WT Smoother and Timestretch stay built in. Copy the nine files from the release zip to the card when you update.
17. [+] **Project Settings page.** File screen F7 `Project Settings` opens one page for the settings of the project: `4 FX Slots` (On or Off), `Macro depth` (see item 13 of Details, Synth), `Strum volume`, `Random seed`, `Reroll seed` and `Scale snap`. It replaces the File screen F7 `Strum ramp` and F8 `4 FX not CE` and the rows `Random seed`, `Reroll seed` and `Scale snap` that were on the Config screen. Back (F7 or Left) returns to the File screen. The `Scale snap` rows of the tracks are the project setting the Snap FX (`N`, item 22 of Details, Tracker) falls back to.
18. [+] **project.mtff.** A new text file next to `project.mt` in the project folder holds the settings of the project that the `.mt` has no room for: the Strum ramp depth, the random seed, send ducking, the groove, scale snap and follow chord of each track and the Parts list. It is the one per project file besides `plugins.txt`. It follows the project through Save, Save As and Open like `plugins.txt`. It also stores the CRC of the `project.mt` it belongs to, so a firmware that does not know the file (or a Mac tool) can change the `.mt` without the settings being applied to the wrong slot. Lines this version does not know are kept when it rewrites the file. The first line names the format version and the firmware version that wrote the file, so a later release can upgrade or downgrade the settings of a project. Opening a project whose file comes from a newer FF shows a short popup once. If the file is of a newer format it is left alone: Save and Save As still save the project, the file stays as it is and FF settings changed in this version are not stored. A project without a setting has no file. A plugin engine reads the depth through a new optional host query (`PLUG_EXT_STRUM`, section 12 of the plugin SDK guide), plugins that do not ask are not affected.
19. [~] **Delay and Reverb Mute save CPU.** On Master > Global Mixer page 3 the Mute keys of the Delay and the Reverb now put the effect and its send mixer to sleep. The tail rings out for up to 3 seconds and fades over about 93 ms, then the effect stops running. A muted effect costs almost nothing and unmuting starts from clean buffers, so the old tail does not come back. With Mute off the audio is bit identical to before.
20. [+] **Chance FX (`K`).** A step FX with the value domain of Chance. When its condition fails the note still plays and only the other FX of the step is skipped (a chord, strum, roll or filter FX on every 4th bar, an accent on 25 % of the steps). Chance still drops the whole step. Projects that use it play the note without the gate on older firmware (the FX id is unknown there).
21. [+] **Long pass conditions for Chance and Chance FX.** `1/8` to `8/8` (pass N of 8, `8/8` is every 8th pass) and `P01` to `P16` (pass N of 16), counted per track like the play/skip cycles. They use the Chance values 175 to 198, older projects never contain them.
22. [+] **Snap FX (`N`).** A step FX that sets the scale snap of its track from that step on: Off, Nearest, Down or Up, whatever the Scale snap row of the Project Settings page says. The fifth value, Track, clears the latch and puts the track back on its project setting from that step. The latch also holds until the next Snap FX on the track or the end of the pattern, then the track goes back to the project setting on its own. The note of its own step is snapped too, in any FX slot and for stutter, arp and roll notes. Play from the cursor and a pattern loop sound the same as playing from the top. A Chance FX that skips its partner slot skips the Snap as well. A pattern without a Snap FX plays as before. Old firmware shows the FX id as unknown FX and does nothing.
23. [+] **Tape (`W` and `Z`).** One step FX for sample and Poly synth tracks. Values 1 to 64 are a Stop (`W`): the track slows to a crawl while the level fades out. Values 65 to 128 are a Start (`Z`): a note spins up from a standstill. The run is 1 to 64 quarters of a step long (4 is one step), for a Start the value minus 64. Turning the value runs from Stop into Start without a gap. Old firmware shows the FX id as an unknown FX.
24. [*] **Export and playback follow a Swing FX or tempo change exactly.** The step clock read the tempo and the swing only once per audio block, so a step that started inside a block kept the old step length until the block ended. A song with a Swing FX ran a little fast, so Export Song and Export Pattern came out 0.04 to 0.1 % too fast (a 125 BPM song with swing 66 measured 125.12 BPM). The clock now reads tempo and swing again after every step. Songs with a Swing FX now export 0.04 to 0.1 % slower than on 1.3.x, which is the correct length (the old tempo was the bug). Songs without Swing FX or tempo changes are unchanged. Reported by TNoize.
25. [+] **Pattern length for each track.** Hold Length (F2) with Shift and the encoder or arrows to set the length of the track under the cursor. The label reads track/pattern, for example `3/8`. The steps past the end of the track are drawn dimmed. A pattern marks itself when it uses this, so patterns of earlier versions play as before.
26. [+] **Follow chord.** Page 3 of the pattern editor (More twice) has Follow (F4, Off, Snap, Transp or Tr+Snap) and Chord (F5, the chord track). A following track plays its notes snapped to the chord of the chord track or transposed to its root or both. Without a setting every track plays as before.
27. [+] **Scale snap.** The row `Scale snap` of the Project Settings page (File screen, F7) sets Off, Nearest, Down or Up for each track. While the sequencer plays the notes of that track move to the project scale (Config > Project Settings, Notes scale and Root note). The pattern data is not changed and live play from the pads or a keyboard is not touched. Off is as before.
28. [+] **Random seed.** The Project Settings page (File screen, F7) has two rows, `Random seed` (Off or 1 to 255) and `Reroll seed`. With a seed the random parts of the sequencer (Chance, the random note, value, volume, instrument and roll FX, the random play mode) play the same take on every Play from the same position and bar 2 still differs from bar 1. Off is as before, a different take each time. The seed is saved with the project.
29. [+] **Quantize with strength, track groove and Replace recording.** The Fill dialog of the Micro-move column has a Quantize type that moves the micromoves toward the grid by a strength in percent (100 puts them on the grid). Page 3 of the pattern editor (More twice) has a Groove value for each track, -50 to +50. A positive value delays the odd steps (swing), a negative value pulls recorded micromoves toward the grid. It is applied at playback only. The pattern keeps its micromoves and 0 is as before. Config > General > Recording mode can be set to Replace, the playhead then clears the armed track while you record and one Undo brings the old content back.
30. [+] **Send ducking.** One source (a track, the line in or a synth part) pulls the delay and reverb returns down while it sounds. Master > Limiter (F4) has a new page on F6, Send Ducking, with Depth, Release, Threshold and Source. The dry audio is not touched and the default is off.
31. [+] **Role fill.** The Fill dialog of the Note column has a new type, Role, that writes a Kick, Snare, Hat, Bass or Lead idea over the selection. Auto gives the selected tracks the roles in order. Bass and Lead follow the Root and Scale of the dialog.
32. [*] **Projects in nested folders.** The paths of `plugins.txt` were built in 64 byte buffers without a length check. A project saved in a folder deeper than about 50 characters wrote the file to a wrong name and two projects with a long common folder path could share one file. The paths now have room for the longest path the file manager builds and a longer one is refused with a save error instead of writing a wrong file.
33. [*] **Project folders too deep for their files.** A project saved in a folder whose path was longer than about 216 characters wrote its pattern, project and instrument files under cut-off names while the save reported success. Save As now refuses such a folder with the New Folder popup before anything is written and Save of a project opened from one goes to Save As.
34. [*] **Sample Loader on an empty or unreadable list.** The Sample Loader read the first character of the name of the selected row without checking that the row has a name. With an empty card, a folder that could not be read or the moment after entering a folder, the buttons were lit as for a file and Preview handed a bad name to the file manager. A row without a name has nothing to enter, add, preview or import now and its buttons are shown inactive. The same round fixes folders such as `Loops (120-140 BPM)` that did not open, the last file of a full folder (101 entries) that could not be selected on its own and a multi-file import that wrote past the last instrument slot. These Sample Loader fixes are by Łukasz Langa, part of the upstream fixes of item 14 of Details, Tracker.
35. [*] **Sample Editor Reverse inside a selection.** Reverse on a selection started writing one sample past the end of the selection, so the first sample of the source was lost and one sample outside the selection was changed. It now writes inside the selection only.
36. [*] **Glue and Phaser fit the insert memory together.** The memory both master inserts run from grew from 8,192 to 9,728 bytes, so every pair of two different inserts of the release fits and the known issue of 1.3.0 (`Full` on the second of Glue and Phaser) is gone. A pair that is still too big, such as Glue twice, shows `Full:<name>` and loads when the other insert leaves room. The memory comes out of the free heap, 1,536 bytes less and nothing else changes.
37. [+] **More on the Features page.** Play stop lock (Play does not stop a running song, Shift + Play does) and MIDI remote (the keys, pads and encoder from MIDI notes and CC 96 and 97 on a chosen channel, off by default). A key or pad held by a note counts as held like a finger's, repeats while held and makes no second press when a finger holds it too. Pulling the USB cable lets go of the keys a note holds.
38. [+] **Plugins that follow the sequencer.** A master insert can ask for the tempo and the beat of the sequencer (`PLUG_EXT_TEMPO`, plugin ABI 1.3) and can run on a silent bus when it makes sound of its own (`PLUG_EXT_TAIL`). Older plugins and hosts are not affected: a plugin without the query is called as before. This is a capability for plugin authors. No insert that uses it ships with this release.
39. [~] **Smaller number printing.** The values the screens print with a decimal point (dB, seconds, tempo, plugin parameters) now come from a small printf of our own (OpenTracker's, NOTICE item 23) instead of newlib's. The text is the same character for character and the image is about 4 KB shorter.
40. [+] **Smaller changes.** The Insert key on a synth patch value cycles the middle, maximum and minimum of its range. Choosing Don't save when you open or start another project keeps the discarded session as `Workspace.prev` on the card (one generation). You can restore it by renaming it to `Workspace`.
41. [!] A project that uses FX3 and FX4 is silent in those two slots on firmware 1.3.x and on stock CE and loses their content when it is saved there with Save As. The switch is Off for new projects. See item 1 of Details, Tracker.
42. [!] The nine Sample Editor effects that moved to the card (item 16 of Details, Tracker) need their `.ptfx` files in `/Effects/`. Without a file the row reads `Missing: <name>` and the effect cannot be applied.
43. [!] MOD import, IT import and MOD/IT export need `/Plugins/Ext/modit.plg` from the same release zip. A file of another release is refused (item 15 of Details, Tracker).
44. [!] USB audio in 8+3 mono mode costs CPU while the computer records: about 180 microseconds more per audio block than Stereo (measured on a unit). A very busy project can overrun a block while recording in 8+3. Stereo mode is not affected.
45. [!] Shift + Power screenshots can freeze the Tracker (known since 1.2.0). Hold the power button to switch it off and on again.

#### Synth

1. [+] **Synth play page.** Shift + Perform opens a page that plays one synth part from a keyboard or a DAW on USB or DIN, with the song stopped (a running song asks first: Cancel or Stop). The columns are Instrument, Voices (up to 8), Velocity curve (Soft, Linear, Hard or Fixed), Bend range (0 to 12 semitones, 2 by default), Transpose, Mod wheel (CC 1 to one of the six macros), Sustain (CC 64) and Channel. Shift + F8 opens the settings: Input (USB and DIN, USB or DIN) and the fixed velocity. CC 120, 121 and 123 work, Shift + Delete is the panic, the pads play the part. Play, Rec and the external Start, Continue, Stop and Song Position do nothing there, MIDI clock is still counted. Any page key leaves it: every note is released, the voice pool goes back to 4 and the project is as it was. The settings are kept in `/Features/synthplay.cfg`. A part on the page takes up to 8 voices. Outside the page a synth instrument still takes up to 4 voices on every engine as in 1.3.0.
2. [+] **A/B on the Synth play page.** A second synth part (Synth B) can play the same keys as the first, up to 8 voices on each side. Morph crossfades between the two parts (equal power, 50 by default). Bank A and Bank B step the patch slot of each part while held notes keep sounding. Perform pressed a third time opens the page too (Shift + Perform opens it from every other page, on Performance it keeps flipping the track pages), a fourth press goes back to Performance page 1. The page keeps its state when you leave and come back to the same instrument: Synth A and B, Morph, the banks, Transpose and the selected column. Press a Bank column again to step on to its Delay and Reverb sends (Bank A, Delay A, Reverb A, then Bank A again, the same for B), 0 to 100, the values of the Instrument page. REC on the page records a mono take of the master output as heard into the free sample memory and lands it as a new instrument named `AB_NNN` in the first free slot after the last used one (the take stops by itself when the sample memory is full). PLK plays 4+4 on the page, every other engine 8+8. Needs CHIP 1.1.1 (`chip.plg`, 29,372 B), which plays 16 voices and keeps the sound of 1.1.0. B and Morph are not stored in the project.
3. [+] **ALCO 1.1.1, a new synth plugin.** `alco.plg` has two oscillators and a sub joined by one of 20 algorithms, with one to three unison oscillators and a chord mode that runs oscillators A and B on every chord note. The wave of an oscillator can be a single cycle wave from `/Waves` on the card. The Wave Mix algorithm blends the two. A patch stores the path of the wave as well as its list index (patch field 101), so the wave stays the right one when files are added to or removed from the card. The firmware answers the path lookup through an optional tail of the files extension of the plugin ABI 1.4 (`wave_ref`, `read_path`, `next_path`, section 12 of the plugin SDK guide). Plugins that do not ask are not affected, a patch without the field plays and saves as before and older firmware keeps the field as an unknown field. ALCO plays a MIDI Chord as paraphonic chord notes and follows the Strum ramp depth of the project. It needs firmware 1.4.0 for the wave mode, because 1.3.x has no files extension (not tried on a unit). Like PHZ and PLK it is a proprietary freeware binary (free use with the Polyend Tracker and tracker-ff, unmodified redistribution, the terms are in the `LICENSE.md` that ships with it), not part of the firmware image and not built from this repository. Copy `alco.plg` with its `.plgi` logo to `/Plugins/Synth/`. A part whose engine is not on the card shows Missing and keeps its settings.
4. [~] **A curated wave set in the release zip and ALCO wave files show a name.** The zip carries a flat `/Waves` folder of 143 single cycle waves from AKWF-FREE by Kristoffer Ekstrand (CC0 1.0): the basic shapes first, then a spread over the AKWF categories. The credit is in `/Waves/AKWF-LICENSE.txt`, the full set of 4,358 waves is at https://github.com/KristofferKarlAxelEkstrand/AKWF-FREE. Waves you already have on the card stay. The A File and B File rows of ALCO's wave mode show the file's name (the base name without `.wav`, a name over 20 characters cut in the middle) and never a number: a row with no file shows `Sine`, which is what ALCO plays for it, a stored name that is not on the card shows `? ` and the name. A card with no `.wav` in `/Waves` does not list the two rows at all (not in the parameter list, the macro targets or the automation destinations), ALCO plays its built-in wave. A patch that stores a subfolder path (`AKWF_bw_sin/AKWF_sin_0001.wav`) finds the same file name directly in `/Waves`.
5. [+] **CHIP 1.1.1.** `chip.plg` has room for 16 voices (8 on each side of the A/B pair on the Synth play page) and renders faster. The sound is the same as 1.0.0 to the last bit, so old projects and patches open as before. For a held 16 note chord on the default patch the estimate is about 280 percent of an audio block before the speed work and about 137 percent after it (an estimate within 40 percent, not measured on a unit). A heavy patch still needs more than one block at 16 voices, so the voice limiter trims it. The file is 29,372 B. The A/B page needs this version for 8+8.
6. [+] **JU6 1.1.0: Para chord mode and Strum.** `ju6.plg` has four new parameters at the end of its list: Chord Mode (Poly or Para), Chord, Voicing and Strum. In Para a note plays as the chord that Chord picks (68 chords, 4 voicings), Strum starts the chord notes one after another and they follow the Strum ramp. Poly is the default and sounds exactly as before. A patch saved with the older file loads unchanged. The new file needs firmware 1.3.0 or newer, on 1.2.x the long Chord list is refused and the JU6 slot reads Missing.
7. [+] **CS-Type 1.0.1.** `cstype.plg` sounds the same as 1.0.0 to the last bit and uses less CPU. On the default patch the estimate is about 10 points of an audio block less at 4 voices (about 25 to 15 percent) and about 21 points less at 8 voices (about 50 to 29 percent), an estimate within 40 percent, not measured on a unit. The file grows from 13,420 B to 24,088 B.
8. [*] **Two tracks on the same pitch.** The sequencer numbers the notes it sends to a synth part now and a note off carries the number of its note. Before, a note off of one track ended every sounding voice of that pitch, so two live notes of the same pitch from two tracks ended together. Notes from the pads and from MIDI in still end by pitch and sound as before. Stop still releases everything.
9. [+] **Morph FX (`o`).** A step FX for synth parts that blends between the open patch slot and a target slot of the Patch FX bank, 0 plays the open slot and 100 the target. The target is the slot of a Patch FX in the other FX column of the step or else a per part Morph target (Shift + the encoder on the slot selector of the Synth screen). The blend stays until another Patch or Morph step changes it. Old firmware plays the step without the FX.
10. [+] **Vary and Randomize for synth patches.** F4 on the synth patch editor moves every continuous value of the patch by up to the shown percent (10 by default), at 100 percent every value is drawn anew. Shift + F4 undoes the last Vary, Shift + the encoder sets the amount in steps of 5. The varied patch is an ordinary edited patch, so Save writes it and leaving without Save keeps the old file.
11. [+] **Strum ramp.** A strummed chord (the Strum FX next to a MIDI Chord) now loses a little level per note in time order and an upstroke is lighter than a downstroke. The depth is a setting of the project, 0 to 100 percent, on the Project Settings page (File screen, F7), row `Strum volume`, in steps of 10. 0 is no ramp and sounds as before 1.4.0. A new project starts at 100. A project from an earlier version has no setting and plays with the ramp off until you turn it on.
12. [+] **Strum from a rolled chord.** When record join writes a chord you played as a roll (the notes on average 15 ms or more apart), the other slot of its MIDI Chord FX pair gets a Strum FX with the speed and the direction of the roll. A busy slot keeps the chord and gets no Strum. Notes pressed together write the chord as before.
13. [+] **Macro depth (`macro.mode`).** New project setting on the Project Settings page: Per patch (the default, every patch plays and loads as before), Add or Scale. With Add or Scale a synth patch that comes into the project later (a new patch, the Init after a Model change and a patch loaded from the library) takes that mode of the macro amount. A patch that is already in the project does not change when the setting changes. The setting is kept in the line `macro.mode` of `project.mtff` and written only for Add and Scale, so a project that does not use it has the file it had before.
14. [+] **ACD (built in).** In Para mode a strummed chord follows the Strum ramp depth of the project: the chord notes lose level in time order and an upstroke is lighter than a downstroke. A ramp of 0 sounds as before. Every Poly patch sounds as before too. The engine writes the left channel only and the part path skips the right channel, with the same sound (see VAP for the figures).
15. [+] **VAP (built in).** The sound is the same as before to the last bit and the part costs less CPU: the engine writes the left channel only, the part path skips the right channel and the voice records sit in the faster memory. Measured on the unit (3 runs per side, the 1.4.0 image against the same image without the synth CPU changes): one voice costs 47 us less per audio block (789 to 742 us), four voices 97 us less (1,111 to 1,014 us). These figures include the changes every synth shares, not the mono path alone.
16. [+] **WTFM (built in).** The same changes as VAP: the sound is the same to the last bit, the engine writes the left channel only and the voice records sit in the faster memory. The saving was not measured on the unit for WTFM alone.
17. [!] Firmware 1.2.0 keeps at most 768 bytes of patch data it does not know. A patch saved by 1.3.0 or later that carries more than 765 bytes of such data fails to load on 1.2.0. Normal patches carry 8 to 52 bytes.
18. [!] The first install of a plugin engine can still cost a short audio gap in a busy project.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-13 - tracker-ff 1.3.0 (original Tracker, MK66)

Version 1.3.0 of the tracker-ff firmware for the original Tracker. The Tracker+ and the Mini are not part of it.

### Added

1. **USB audio.** Built in. With USB audio on, the unit shows up as an audio input on a computer after the next start. With it off, the unit presents only the stock USB interfaces.
2. **Glue master insert.** `glue.plg` in `/Plugins/Master/`, a bus compressor with Auto makeup and a Character setting (Clean, Glue, Smooth, Punch or Colour). Its Makeup reads `Auto` at the bottom step. A plugin can name the end values of a numeric parameter now (`PLUG_PARAM_NAME_MIN` and `PLUG_PARAM_NAME_MAX`), older plugins and hosts are not affected. There are eight inserts now. Glue and Phaser do not fit the insert memory together, the second of the two shows `Full`.
3. **Paraphony.** A synth plugin can answer a chord query of the firmware (an optional extension of the plugin format, older plugins and hosts are not affected). ACD is the one engine of this release with a Para mode. It has three new parameters at the end of its Voice section: Chord Mode (Poly or Para), Chord and Strum. The mode is stored per patch. In Para a MIDI Chord step plays its notes as one voice with extra oscillators through the one filter and the envelopes, which costs less audio CPU than a voice per note. Patches from 1.2.x load with Poly and sound as before. Engines without a Para mode play the chord notes as separate voices. The patch Chord plays for notes from the pads, a MIDI keyboard and plain steps, and a MIDI Chord step FX wins for its own step (the same goes for Strum).
4. **Chord capture and chord names.** Play MIDI keys together with the cursor on a MIDI or synth step (any column), or hold pads with the cursor on a MIDI Chord cell, and the Tracker writes the chord: the lowest note becomes the step note and the chord goes into the FX value. A popup at the cursor shows the chord name and the held notes. A chord that is not an exact match gets the nearest one and a `~` in front of its name (`~Cmaj7`). A group with no near match leaves the step as it was. One Undo takes the chord back. The title bar and the popup show chord names with root and bass such as `Fmaj7/C`, the Fill list shows the name without a root such as `maj7 inv2`. MIDI Chord values above 29 (the inversions) play only the bass note on stock firmware, so a project with captured inversions sounds thinner there.
5. **Strum FX.** `u - Strum` in the FX slot next to a `0 - MIDI Chord` starts the chord notes one after another: 1 to 63 up (the lowest note first), 65 to 127 down, 2 ms between two notes per unit, 0 and 64 off. The notes start on audio blocks of about 2.9 ms, so `+10` is about 20 ms. A Para ACD patch strums inside its voice with its own Strum parameter.
6. **Record join.** Notes you play together while recording are written as one step with a MIDI Chord. With several tracks armed the chord joins onto the track under the cursor.
7. **Synth order.** Shift + F1 on the Model column of the Synth screen edits the engine list: Shift + Up and Down move an engine, Delete hides it, Insert shows it again, and the arrow keys wrap from the last engine to the first. The order is saved in `/Plugins/synth-order.txt`. Projects and `.patch` files still store the engine itself, so nothing in them changes. An engine that the open project plays is shown at the top even when hidden, and an engine without a line in the file is shown at the end.
8. **Macro depth Scale.** A new patch scales a macro amount to the room between the parameter value and its end, so an Amount of 1.00 reaches the maximum and -1.00 the minimum (the Amount is shown from -2.00 to 2.00, 1.00 is 100 %). Patches from 1.2.x keep the old rule. Firmware 1.2.x plays every macro with the old rule, so a 1.3.0 patch that uses a macro amount sounds different there.
9. **Features page.** Config > Features lists the options of the firmware that have a switch of their own. Each one has a State row and a page with its settings, and the settings are kept per feature in `/Features/` on the card. Reset config clears that folder. A feature that needs a restart says so and offers Restart now. The page lists USB audio and Chord names. Chord names switches the MIDI Chord cells of the pattern grid between short names such as `m7` and the interval digits.
10. **Note-on admission.** A synth note whose estimated cost would push the audio block over its time budget (87 % of the block) starts at the start of the next block, at most one block late and never dropped. A voice that a new note takes over in a full pool fades out in the block of the note, and a released voice that is near silence is freed at once.

Items 3 to 6 and 8 (paraphony, chord capture and chord names, Strum, record join and macro depth) are explained in the user guide [`docs/chords_and_macros.md`](docs/chords_and_macros.md). Item 7 (Synth order) is explained in the user guide [`docs/synth_order.md`](docs/synth_order.md).

### Changed

1. **Sample input chain idles.** The USB input stream of the original sends no data, and a gate whose pre-FX mixer inputs are silenced builds no output block, so an unused input chain costs less CPU. The output is unchanged.
2. **CHIP is an installable engine.** CHIP is no longer built into the image. It is `chip.plg` in `/Plugins/Synth/` like the other plugin engines, and it frees about 15 KB of flash. The sound, the parameters and the numbers are unchanged, so old projects and `.patch` files open on it as before. A part on CHIP shows `Missing: CHIP` on a card without the plugin. When you update from 1.2.x, copy `chip.plg` to `/Plugins/Synth/` too.
3. **Patch FX.** `O - Patch` now applies the macros and the automation of the slot it switches to, together with its values. An LFO or envelope that sits on the same slot type in both patches keeps running, one that changes type starts new and an automation slot that is Off reads zero.
4. **Model list rest.** Every step onto another engine in the Model list now restarts the 1 s rest, built-in engines included. Before, only a step onto an engine that was not installed waited, so scrolling with the arrows, their key repeat or the encoder picked each built-in engine on the way.
5. **Transient Shaper.** The sample editor effect has new envelope times, and its Sustain gain is held back while a hit is rising.
6. **Add to Empty Slot.** With files selected in the Sample Loader, Shift with the add button adds the selection and does not jump to the first empty slot. After a jump the instrument list and the memory bar refresh.
7. **Short delay.** The delay runs only the lines of the mode that is on (mono or ping pong), which uses less audio CPU. The sound is unchanged while the mode stays. Switching the mode with echoes ringing now crossfades over about 90 ms and carries the echoes over, where it used to cut to unrelated echoes with a click.
8. **Master insert 2 runs from the shared SRAM slot.** Both master inserts run from one 8 KB internal SRAM slot, insert 1 from the bottom and insert 2 from the top, so insert 2 no longer writes to the data flash. A plugin that fits alone but not beside the other insert shows `Full:<name>` and loads when the other one leaves room. The synth plugin bank takes the two freed sectors and now has all 32 sectors of the data flash.
9. **Lower audio CPU beyond the delay.** The reverb loop runs at half rate with the same room size, damping and decay time, and its lines fit internal SRAM, so the 32 KB SDRAM buffer is gone. The limiter keeps its state in registers and divides only above the threshold. A sounding synth part skips the parameter refresh when nothing changed. The synth sends are skipped when both pre-FX send gains are 0. The sample voice loops keep their state in registers. The internal sequencer clock counts in integers. All of these give the same output bit for bit.

### Fixed

1. Installing a synth plugin into the data flash no longer makes the audio crackle. The audio output renders ahead into a deeper buffer while the flash erases, and the erase works in short slices.
2. The delay feedback and the reverb damping are set the same way at the start as a project load sets them. Before, they started from the raw project values and the first project load corrected them.
3. The master inserts of a project load now wait like the synth engines do: 1 s after the load finished, then one insert at a time, 250 ms apart. Before, both read the card in the first main loop pass after the load. A pick on the Inserts page still loads after 400 ms.
4. A part whose engine is not on the card (for example CHIP without `chip.plg`) keeps that engine. The encoder, the arrow keys and F1 on its Model list show the `not installed` popup and change nothing, and no row of the list is highlighted. Before, one encoder click turned the part into ACD, and the list highlighted ACD so that Delete in the edit mode hid ACD.

### Known issues

1. A patch saved by 1.3.0 with a macro amount in Scale mode plays with the old Add rule on firmware 1.2.x, so that macro sounds different there.
2. Macro depth mode is stored per patch and the unit has no row to switch it yet. New patches are Scale, patches without the mode stored are Add.
3. Glue and Phaser do not fit the insert memory together. The second of the two shows `Full` and passes the audio.
4. A part whose engine is missing still opens its Macros and Automation pages, and edits made there are not kept.
5. Firmware 1.2.0 keeps at most 768 bytes of patch data it does not know. A patch saved by 1.3.0 that carries more than 765 bytes of such data fails to load on 1.2.0. Normal patches carry 8 to 52 bytes.
6. The first install of a plugin engine can still cost a short audio gap in a busy project. The gap is much shorter than in 1.2.0.
7. Shift + Power screenshots can freeze the Tracker (known since 1.2.0). Hold the power button to switch it off and on again.

Updating from 1.1.0 or 1.2.0: see [`UPGRADING.md`](UPGRADING.md). `chip.plg` has to be on the card for projects that use CHIP.

1. **Lower audio CPU.** The synth engine code is built with `-O2` (FM4 and B-Type `-O3`) and without fused multiply and add steps, which are faster on the Tracker's processor. The sound of a note can differ from earlier builds in its exact waveform.
2. **Plugins load safely.** A marker on the card keeps a crashing plugin from loading at the next start, and holding Shift in the first 4 seconds after power on starts without plugins.

4. The playhead stays inside very short backward, ping-pong and granular loops, wavetable and granular position modulation clamps at 0, and song blocks smaller than 32 steps copy and paste correctly.
5. The CHIP engine no longer overloads the audio interrupt with many voices.
6. Changing the engine of a synth part, loading a project, saving a recording, listing a folder and opening the value list of a synth parameter no longer write through a null pointer when the heap is short. Large synth blocks come from the external memory and a failed allocation ends the action with an error.
7. The synth patch editor refuses a parameter or macro index outside the patch.
8. Power off always switches the Tracker off. Before, it could hang at the end with the screen on and every key dead. The wait for a running card operation is now bounded, a short heap no longer faults while the shutdown bar is created and a software deadline switches the Tracker off if the sequence stalls.

## [1.0.0] - Initial Community Edition Release

> **⚠️ Important :: Project Compatibility Notice:**
> To support dedicated per-step **Micro-move (`m`)** and **Volume (`V`)** columns alongside **`Fx1`** and **`Fx2`**, pattern files (`.mtp`) have been updated to a new format (**Version 6**).
> 
> - **Loading old projects works automatically**: Existing projects created on official/older firmwares load seamlessly and are upgraded in memory.
>   
> - **Saving updates the project to Version 6 (not backward-compatible with older firmware)**: Once you save a project on Community Edition firmware, its saved `.mtp` pattern files cannot be read by older firmwares. **Please make a backup of your `Projects/` folder on your SD card or use `Save As` to save under a new project name, if you ever plan to roll back to an older firmware.**

### Added

1. **Open Synth SDK & New `CHIP` + `DIRT` Synthesizer Engines (Tracker+ & Tracker Mini)**
   * Added a new Open Synth SDK allowing the community to add new synthesizer engines. `CHIP` is the first implementation of this open architecture.
   * Added **`DIRT`** (wave-shaping/distortion synth).
   * See [`docs/custom_synth_sdk.md`](docs/custom_synth_sdk.md) to learn more about developing your own engines.

2. **Modular Sample Editor Effect & Generator Plugins (All 3 Devices)**
   * Added hot-swappable DSP plugins loaded from `/Effects/*.ptfx` on the SD card. 
   * Supports up to **12 parameters** (across 3 pages) and up to **128 installed plugins**.
   * Added the complete `.ptfx` SDK, `ptfx_builder.py`, and 6 reference effects/generators (`Add Silence`, `Analog Kick Gen`, `Chiptune Voice Gen`, `Multi-Mode Filter`, `Stereo Delay`, `Stereo to Mono`) in [`editor-fx-sdk/`](editor-fx-sdk/).
   * See [`docs/modular_effects_system.md`](docs/modular_effects_system.md) to learn more about developing your own plugins.

3. **Wavetable Envelopes (`WT Env`), Wavetable LFOs (`WT LFO`) & Step FX (`w` / `y`) (All 3 Devices)**
   * Added `WT Env` and `WT LFO` modulation types across all 7 instrument parameters (`Volume`, `Panning`, `Cutoff`, `Wavetable Position`, `Granular Position`, `Granular Length`, `Finetune`) with **128 Factory (`F 001`–`F 128`)** and **128 User (`U 001`–`U 128`)** morphable contours and dual-axis `Warp X/Y` shaping.
   * Load your own wavetables to overwrite the 128 User shapes, by uploading them to `/Envelopes/*.wav`
   * Added `w` (WT Volume Env) and `y` (WT Filter Env) pattern step FX for per-step envelope duration/warp/ADSR shaping.
   * See [`docs/wavetable_envelopes.md`](docs/wavetable_envelopes.md).

4. **Custom Color Themes & Font Packages (All 3 Devices)**
   * You can now create your own themes and load them from `/Themes/*.json` on your SD Card (up to 29 custom themes alongside the 3 built-in themes) and custom antialiased font packages from `/Fonts/*.ptfont` (up to 30 custom fonts) selectable in `Config > General > Theme / Font`.
   * Create your own with the [Web Theme & Font Editor](https://polyend.github.io/tracker-theme-editor/) ([`polyend/tracker-theme-editor`](https://github.com/polyend/tracker-theme-editor)). For more information see: [`docs/custom_themes_and_fonts.md`](docs/custom_themes_and_fonts.md).

5. **Performance Mode Enhancement (All 3 Devices)**
   * Added page toggling between Sample Tracks and MIDI/Synth Tracks in Performance Mode. Just keep pressing `[Perform]` to toggle between the views.
   * Oh, for OG Tracker users, there's a small surprise that goes along well with this change.

6. **Hybrid SD Card Sample Streaming (All 3 Devices)**
   * Long samples  (`256 KB` and larger) now keep only their initial attack in sample memory while streaming the rest of the audio directly from the SD card in the background. This lets you load a massive amount of stems, loops, and recordings into a single project while using only a fraction of your sample RAM.
     
   * **What Streams on Each Device**:
     * **Tracker+ & Tracker Mini**: Streams **`One-Shot`**, **`Forward Loop`**, **`Backward Loop`**, **`Ping-Pong Loop`**, **`Slice`**, and **`Beat Slice`** instruments (`Wavetable` and `Granular` instruments remain fully in RAM).
       
     * **Original Tracker**: Streams **`One-Shot`**, **`Forward Loop`**, **`Backward Loop`**, and **`Ping-Pong Loop`** instruments, while keeping **`Slice`** and **`Beat Slice`** instruments (alongside `Wavetable` and `Granular`) fully in RAM to keep performance solid across multiple tracks.
       
   * **Works Transparently with the Sample Editor**: You can still edit streamed samples in the Sample Editor or switch play modes at any time. The Tracker automatically loads the full sample into RAM while you edit it and returns to streaming once your edits are applied. This excludes very long sample however, due to RAM restrictions. If you plan to stream multi-minutes samples, please prepare them outside of the device.
   * See [`docs/developer_guide.md`](docs/developer_guide.md#3-copy-on-write-workspaceinstruments-incremental-project-saver--hybrid-sd-streaming) for technical details.

7. **`Slice` & `Beat Slice` Instruments can now `Play Thru` (All 3 Devices)**
   * Added a per-instrument **`Play Thru`** (`Off` / `On`) setting on the **`More`** page of the **Sample Playback** screen for both **`Slice`** and **`Beat Slice`** modes.
   * When **`Play Thru`** is **`On`**, triggering any slice starts at that slice marker and continues playing through subsequent slices until the note is released (`Note Off` / `CUT` / `FAD`) or the end of the sample is reached.

8. **Dedicated Micro-move (`m`) & Volume (`V`) Step View (All 3 Devices)**
   * Each pattern step now stores dedicated **Micro-move (`m`)** (`0..100`) and **Volume / Velocity (`V`)** (`0..100`) values in addition to its two custom step FX slots (**`Fx1`** and **`Fx2`**), freeing both FX slots for creative use.
   * Pressing **`[Note]`** in Pattern View while the cursor is already on the **`Note`** column toggles the two effect columns between the standard **`[Fx1 | Fx2]`** view and the new **`[m | V]`** view.
   * Unquantized live recording (**Record Micro-move**) and velocity recording (**Record Velocity**) now write directly into the dedicated **`m`** and **`V`** step fields instead of occupying **`Fx1`** and **`Fx2`**.
   * Full support in **`[m | V]`** view for Jog-wheel / `±` editing, Pad entry, `Delete`, `Copy` / `Paste`, `Invert`, `Undo` / `Redo`, and the **`Fill`** tool (`Constant`, `From-To`, `Random`, `Euclidean`).

9. **Granular Length Automation & Step FX (`z`) (All 3 Devices)**
   * Added **`Granular Length`** as a modulatable parameter in **Instrument Automation**, allowing you to animate grain size over time using an Envelope, LFO and the new Wavetable Envelope or LFOs.
   * Added the **`z`** step FX (`0`–`100%`) to control grain length on a per-step basis (`0–50%` gives fine `1.0–100.0 ms` control for tight micro-grains, while `50–100%` sweeps `100.0–1000.0 ms` for lush clouds).
     
10. **More Chance Conditions (All 3 Devices)**
   * Spiced up the **`Chance` (`C`)** step FX beyond percentage probability (`C  0`–`C100`) with conditional and cycle-based modes inspired by the Polyend Play:
     * **Group with Last (`C GL`)**: Plays only if the previous conditional step evaluated on the same track played.
     * **Not Last (`C !L`)**: Plays only if the previous conditional step evaluated on the same track was skipped.
     * **Play N / Skip M (`C1:1`–`C6:6`)**: Plays the step for `N` consecutive pattern playthroughs, then skips it for `M` playthroughs (`N, M` in `1..6`).
     * **Skip N / Play M (`CS11`–`CS66`)**: Skips the step for `N` consecutive pattern playthroughs, then plays it for `M` playthroughs (`N, M` in `1..6`).
     
11. **Reactivated Multi-Folder SD Card Image Viewer (All 3 Devices)**
   * Reactivated and expanded the JPEG image viewer under `Config > Info` with three user folders (`Reference`, `Notes`, and `Custom` in `/Images/<Folder>/1.jpg` … `48.jpg`), supporting Left/Right, Jog-wheel, and direct Pad `1–48` selection.
   * See [`docs/image_viewer.md`](docs/image_viewer.md).

### Changed & Fixed

 * **Live Recording Improvements (All 3 Devices)**:
	* You can now play much tigther notes on single-armed tracks. As in: If you play a second note before releasing the first, it won't get lost.
	* Audible first note after preroll, and configurable first-note recording timing threshold (if you tend to play early).
	  
* **Exact `44100 Hz` Offline Audio Export (All 3 Devices)**:
	* Fixed sample rate drift during offline master and stem export so rendered `.wav` files match real-time `44100 Hz` playback pitch and timing.
	* Also removed excessive silence at end of renders.
	  
* **Removed clicks on rendered samples and editor effects (All 3 devices)**
	* No more clicks at the end of your samples.
	  
* **MIDI Overhaul (All 3 devices)**
	* Fixed USB MIDI enumeration for Windows 11 (March 2026 update).
	* Fixed long-term tempo drift and overhauled how MIDI Clock is sent out. Timing is now ~15× tighter and stays locked over hours of playback.
	* Fixed an issue where the Tracker could start slightly late (by one clock tick) when pressing Play on an external master clock or DAW. Step 1 now fires dead-on the downbeat.
	* The Tracker now responds to MIDI `Continue` and `Song Position Pointer` messages.
	* Improved how the Tracker follows incoming MIDI Clock when your computer or master device sends slightly uneven clock bursts, and fixed the `Clock Sync Delay` setting so fine-tuning your setup's latency works predictably.
	* Fixed low-level USB and TRS MIDI buffer issues that could occasionally drop clocks, notes, or CC automation during busy patterns.
	
* **Faster Project Loading & Saving (All 3 Devices)**:
	* Opening a project no longer duplicates all of its audio samples into a temporary folder on the SD card before loading them into memory. Instead, samples are loaded directly from your project folder, making even large, sample-heavy projects open in a fraction of the time while drastically reducing SD card wear.
	* Saving changes to your current project now only writes out samples whose audio was actually edited or newly added, while pattern edits and instrument parameter tweaks save almost instantly. (`Save As` still saves a complete, standalone copy of your project and all its samples).
	  
* **Major Internal Memory, SDRAM & Audio Engine Optimization (All 3 Devices)**:
	* Reclaimed internal SRAM and SDRAM across all three devices to power Hybrid SD Card Sample Streaming while keeping 100% of the original sample RAM pool intact (`11.25 MB` on Original Tracker / `60 MB` on Tracker+ & Tracker Mini), leaving generous internal RAM headroom (`~128 KB` free on Original Tracker, `~22.5 KB` `SRAM_ITC` + `~21.5 KB` `SRAM_DTC` + `~9.3 KB` `SRAM_OC` on Tracker+ & Tracker Mini) and up to `~29 MB` (`IMX`) / `~5.3 MB` (`MK66`) of reclaimable SDRAM for future community ideas.
	* Optimized real-time audio rendering for cleaner playback on heavy multi-track projects.
	  
* **Open-Source Build Consolidation (All 3 Devices)**:
	* Unified `core/`, `platforms/mk66/`, `platforms/imx/`, `synth/`, `util/`, and `editor-fx-sdk/` into a single monorepo built via a top-level [`Makefile`](Makefile) and [`version.mk`](version.mk).
	* Removed the proprietary NES emulator and repurposed its `EXTERNAL_RAM` region (`externalRamScratchBuff`, `768 KB`) as the shared scratch pool for `.ptfx` plugins, offline audio export, SD `.wav` preview, and modal file browsers. (Sorry gamers).
